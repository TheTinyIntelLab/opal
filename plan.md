# Opal research plan after external validation

Updated 21 October 2026. This is the sole authoritative forward plan. Frozen protocols and dated reports record evidence, not parallel backlogs. The previous plan is preserved in [the archive](docs/frontier/historical/plan-before-research-reset-20261020.md.txt). Keep historical models, negative results, provenance and reproducibility assets.

## Decision and first capability

Narrow Opal to **assay-conditioned prediction of unseen genetic-perturbation pairs from measured singles and a declared amount of local pair supervision**. Retain original completion as an original-screen baseline. Close unchanged-weight cross-screen superiority and reject a universal rescue through substituting local singles. Do not launch a larger expression architecture or wider completion sweep.

The first falsifiable capability is lower held-pair growth error than strong calibrated-single and gene-main-effect predictors on a second source, with local pair-training access disclosed. This validates a frozen learning procedure after adaptation, not zero-shot transfer of original weights. Predicting an entire unobserved pair block is a separate development requirement. Cold genes, unseen contexts without adaptation, expression distributions and mechanistic virtual cells remain unestablished.

This is preferable to an immediate expression pivot because growth has 81,466 eligible pairs, residual alignment survives opposite-infection scoring, and expression composition already failed on sparse exposed sources. It is preferable to increasing completion capacity because assay semantics and graph support remain unresolved, and the frozen correction harms two later cohorts even after local-single substitution. A calibrated simple predictor is an acceptable result if interactions add no robust value.

## Missing-block continuation

The [registered six-task block experiment](docs/research_reset/block_results.md) now gives stronger development support for local pair supervision. Rank-eight completion lowers held-block mean-target MSE versus the validation-selected twelve-reference simple policy by 28.0750% K562, 13.3275% Jurkat, 32.9348% environmental 1, 35.8852% environmental 2, 31.6437% PARPi 2022 and 26.0711% PARPi 2023. Every task clears the 5% margin, negative upper conditional gene bound, both infection-target checks, all-reference veto and three shuffled-label controls. These are complete missing submatrices among training-supported genes, with no test labels used for checkpoint choice. They are previously exposed sources, not fresh confirmation or cold-gene evidence. This favors retaining assay-local completion as a baseline and qualifying a frozen learning procedure on an independent source before adding capacity. The completed native comparison beats the registered simple policy in every task, but has lower MSE than completion only on PARPi 2022 (4.211333%); three of the other five conditional comparison intervals cross zero. The frozen validation-family policy chooses completion in 27 folds and native in 3, improving completion by 2.441797% in environment2 and 1.017655% in PARPi 2022, with identical predictions elsewhere. Only the latter changed-task conditional bound excludes zero. This is exposed development, with no model promotion; [native/family results](docs/research_reset/native_results.md) preserve the complete comparison and costs.

The source audit also limits SPIDR's intended primary both-mismatched-guide stratum. Only nine genes have two mismatched guides; requiring at least two ordered constructs leaves 2,196 designed pairs, all incident to those nine hubs, before observed-count/single QC. The old primary cannot support broad 249-gene pair coverage. The later all-stratum metadata audit therefore supersedes it prospectively with the better-supported perfect/perfect primary described below; no response-dependent choice occurred. Source metadata exposure can support a new prospective registration; it cannot make any already exposed outcome fresh.

### Metrics, dependence and gates

Primary loss is equal-canonical-pair MSE on mean replicate targets, separately per source/context. Secondary outputs include individual infection/culture MSE, MAE, signed bias, centered Pearson, target variance, residual MSE, count/guide/mismatch strata and coverage. Do not pool absolute MSE across gamma/LFC/expression. Any across-task summary uses training-only variance scaling and equal task weights.

Compare paired losses on identical pairs. First-wave node/gene intervals are conditional sensitivity bounds without guaranteed population coverage. Q2/Q6 use 2,000 endpoint-multiplicity gene draws, seed 20261022, reporting discarded empty draws and leave-one-gene-out influence. Pair bootstrap is secondary. Show source/culture effects explicitly. Hierarchical biological uncertainty requires enough actual independent initiations/sources; otherwise report insufficient replication rather than resample guides as cultures.

For future shuffled-residual controls, freeze three seeds, 20261023, 20261024 and 20261025. Keep the training graph fixed and permute only training residual labels after fitting its selected nuisance reference. Stratify edges by the unordered pair of endpoint training-degree tertiles, the unordered pair of endpoint absolute-single-effect tertiles, and minimum construct count bins 2–3, 4–7 and at least 8. Gene cutpoints use active training genes; single-effect summaries average available source contexts/replicates, without pair outcomes. Shuffle within each stratum and use the same pair permutation across jointly available contexts. Singletons stay unchanged. Fit identical rank/penalty/checkpoint procedures to all three controls and require the aligned candidate to beat every control, with no seed selection. This tests alignment beyond coarse nuisance strata; it does not isolate biological interaction or guarantee preservation of every gene-specific error.
