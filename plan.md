# Opal forward plan

Updated 22 October 2026 after wave four. This is the sole authoritative forward
plan. Frozen protocols and reports record completed or stopped work, not competing
backlogs. The exact prior plan is preserved in
[the wave-four archive](results/research-wave4/plan-before-wave4.md.txt).

## Decision

Retain assay-local dense completion as an exposed development baseline. Close
unchanged-weight external superiority, the two registered low-label budgets and
architecture/rank/budget rescue sweeps. No model is promoted in this wave. The
current evidence does not identify biological interactions or establish reliable
independent-source prediction. Frontier perturbation biology and virtual-cell
modeling remain long-term ambitions.

The narrower capability under investigation is prediction of unmeasured pairs
among supported genes, using measured singles and explicitly disclosed local
pair supervision. Adaptation on another source is a procedure-replication task,
not transfer of original weights. Cold genes, unseen contexts without adaptation,
expression distributions, biological prediction intervals and measured assay
savings remain unestablished.

## Next authorized scientific decision

The requested strong-reference discriminator has no completed result. Its two
stopped attempts used the same prespecified scientific recipe, with the successor
changing numerical implementation only. V2's fitted baseline coefficients,
centering and scales match Q2b exactly, but its validation prediction evaluation
path differs by about 1.25e-8, failing the frozen 1e-12 requirement. Honor the
registered second-failure stop. Do not reinterpret that software failure as
support for or against completion, and do not launch another biological fit,
relax the threshold or select a more favorable nuisance representation here.

Wave four reproduced the exact 1.245199382493567e-8 discrepancy. Coordinates,
distances and kernels match; kernel-whitener multiplication first differs by
3.045525368217594e-11, amplified by small saved feature scales. Causal feature
swaps reproduce the failed gate, while addition grouping contributes only about
1e-16. The historical predictor itself varies with caller partitioning. A tested
fixed-order contraction misses the immutable validation vector by
4.660858310412408e-8. The cause is identified on the inspected stack, but no
repair meets the frozen compatibility and invariance requirements. No new
strongest-reference scientific experiment is justified or authorized.

The next numerical decision must explicitly choose a prospective replay contract
before implementation. Preserving a fixed legacy query schedule and defining a
new caller-invariant predictor are different versioned engineering questions.
Do not change the historical target, floors, tolerance or fitted state to turn
either into another scientific retry. Historical source bytes and scores remain
unchanged. Any later scientific continuation still requires a separately
justified registration and independent reviews, with the same population,
comparison, threshold and information budget.

If a qualified nuisance comparison removes completion's advantage, simplify the
predictor and close its biological-interaction interpretation. If an advantage
survives, conclude only that the tested representation did not explain it, and
freeze the procedure for a separately qualified source-level test. Fixed guides
alias reagent and gene effects. An arbitrary technical canonical-pair effect
cannot be separated from a biological interaction using these measurements;
held-block accuracy, shuffled controls and infection agreement do not identify
its cause.

## Source qualification boundary

SPIDR remains a CRISPRi candidate. Its normalized-count endpoint is
T14/T0 LFC, not measured per-doubling gamma. The perfect/perfect primary is a
metadata-only role, with 121,198 two-construct designed pairs across 548 genes;
observed eligibility remains unknown. Mixed and both-mismatched are secondary and
cannot rescue a failed primary. All variants and orientations of a gene pair
share one assignment. Historical activity/GEMINI/GI values and outcome-selected
guide filters are prohibited as priors or eligibility choices.

Before any reserved response or header access, require all of the following:

1. Obtain a response-free Table 8 sample/column and ordered-construct key map,
   actual normalization invocation/reference-set semantics and NT centering.
   Do not round normalized values, normalize twice or import the raw-unit T0
   threshold. Keep the incompatible raw-integer adapter separate.
2. Verify supplement-specific rights with attribution and any item exception,
   exact guide sequence/transcript/mismatch/arm matching, explicit control roles,
   culture/time mapping and initiation lineage. Two split cultures after one
   transduction are not independent initiations. FASTQ rights do not block a
   supplement-only route.
3. Register and test a normalized-unit endpoint and QC/missingness contract.
   Match singles to exact guide/arm/time/culture target-NT constructs, never
   derive them from held doubles. Predict before equal-construct/equal-culture
   aggregation, score pairs equally and report each eligibility stage. Preserve
   zero late counts and disclose early-survival selection.
4. Qualify identical-information reference/completion interfaces, leakage tests,
   strict artifact validation, deterministic replay and a full-design resource
   rehearsal. The synthetic evaluator audit is insufficient for count ingestion.
   State one feasible source-local train-plus-validation budget and its actual
   unique-label union, including separately charged singles and controls.
5. Freeze the learning procedure, endpoint, splits, reference menu, primary MSE,
   conditional uncertainty, stopping rules and promotion gate before opening.
   Save all validation choices and predictions before sealed test scoring. Any
   schema access requiring response values is partial exposure, never blindness.

## Evaluation and delivery rules

Ranked next decisions are: first define and review a versioned numerical replay
contract without a biological fit; second obtain Shen's response-free ordered
construct/sample/initiation map and exact supplement-item rights, because its
dense design could support a separate knockout task; third obtain SPIDR's actual
schema/invocation companion for a prospectively restricted within-initiation
question. Neither source is qualified today. A future sealed protocol must bind
endpoint/QC, exact singles/control matching, source-local unique-label budget,
train-only preprocessing, validation selection, all predictions and the unchanged
scientific gate before custodian scoring. An observed-support check belongs to
authorized nonadaptive ingestion after those prerequisites, not a hidden header
inspection now. No further rank, architecture, budget or nuisance sweep follows
the current stops.

Use identical test pairs, labels and permitted measurements across comparators.
TRAIN alone fits transforms; VALIDATION alone selects penalties/checkpoints.
Held genes exclude every incident outcome from both partitions. Supported lookup
genes and cold genes are different tasks, and source-keyed hashing is not source
holdout. A complete missing submatrix does not prove arbitrary cold-gene transfer.

Retain the historical screening gate unless a new prospective question explicitly
changes it: at least 5% lower paired MSE in every named task, negative upper
conditional gene sensitivity bound, no infection loss, informative performance
against all three shuffled controls and the all-reference point-estimate veto.
Do not count bootstrap genes, guides or split cultures as new biological
replication. Synthetic coverage under shared cohort/global effects demonstrates
why these bounds cannot be described as population-calibrated confidence.

Bind code, inputs, splits, dependencies and registrations before mandatory tests
and fits. Refuse overwrite/drift, preserve interruptions/failures, measure active
wall/CPU/peak memory/output size, and stop at registered caps. Distinguish
cooperative guards from interrupting watchdogs. No larger menu follows a failed
gate. Invalid-checkpoint probes are defects, not evidence of corrupted historical
states; use the tested strict loader in new audit paths without rewriting frozen
modules.

Delivery includes the full suite, wheel/sdist, installed original/external/block/
native/reagent/flexible/budget replays, synthetic series v1/v2, count-assay rehearsal,
docs/link inspection and exclusion of restricted assets/credentials. Record
package-only dependency reuse and all failed checks. Normal origin/main push is
authorized by the repository convention and current task; verify remote HEAD,
clean tracked working tree and hosted Linux/Windows Python 3.11/3.14 CI.
