## Prospective protocol and useful effect

[The prospective protocol](docs/frontier/prospective_confirmation_protocol.md) tests a source-nominated, frozen Opal procedure against a strong same-information source-locked comparator in fresh culture/reference blocks of two already observed contexts. Primary loss is full-response gene MSE averaged over fixed pairs; primary paired improvement is computed at the independent block level, separately by context. It does not establish biological interaction recovery, mechanism, unseen-context transfer or new-pair identity generalization.

One proposed block contains a pair-outcome culture with its own matched vehicle plus two separately initiated control/single reference cultures. The six-intervention/fifteen-pair example costs 30 measured outcomes and three initiations per block; n blocks in each of two contexts cost 60n outcomes and 6n initiations before failures/repeats. This corrects earlier sketches that omitted the separate pair-culture vehicle and conflated reference partitions with initiations. Actual identities, n, QC, rights and quotes remain unset. No lab has agreed and nothing is commissioned.

The biological collaborator must justify a useful absolute improvement margin before confirmation outcomes. Independent nonquery pilot covariance must estimate paired culture/reference variability. [The analytic power sensitivity](results/opal_evidence_audit_v1/confirmation-power-sensitivity-20261015.json) uses an explicit iid-normal paired-block model, one-sided alpha .0125 per context and both contexts required. At standardized excess-over-margin .5, .9 power/context requires 53 blocks/context, 3,180 outcomes and 318 initiations; at excess 1 it requires 16, 960 and 96. These are hypothetical scenarios, not empirical power, selected sample sizes or approved budgets. Pilot uncertainty, nonnormality, attrition, null/coverage validation and actual costs must determine a defensible design. If no affordable useful precision exists, stop.

The protocol freezes source-only nomination/baseline selection, reference information, predictions/inference banks, blinding, complete row accounting, missingness and fallback rules before fresh evaluator access. Both adjusted useful lower bounds and operational integrity are required for success. Adequate precise exclusion of a useful gain is failure for the procedure/population; broad bounds or excess missingness are inconclusive. No outcome-driven rank, sample-size, endpoint or baseline rescue is allowed.
