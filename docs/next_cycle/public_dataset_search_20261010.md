# Internet search continuation, 10 October 2026

The search extended beyond the previous recent-series inventory into older donor studies, yeast culture compendia, L1000 combination resources and newly published Perturb-seq methods. It found a usable single-pair primary-donor workaround, [GSE78083](donor_monocyte_diagnostic_20261010.md), but no accepted original confirmation bank. This is a bounded review, not an exhaustive absence claim.

Four additional series received sample-level SOFT review. GSE293966 is a THP-1 line with priming/time conditions and incomplete three-repeat groups, despite the general monocyte title. GSE277665 has one DOT1L/EZH2 pair in four cell lines with three repeats; its EPZ/EZP reagent spellings need source reconciliation. GSE290240 mixes chemical and genetic CDK2 interventions with JQ1 across cell lines and batches, with three reported biological replicates. GSE328457 mixes GEX, TF-dialout libraries, enrichment populations, differentiation days and higher-order transcription-factor combinations; library counts do not establish complete independent pair panels. Their expression matrices remain unopened.

Two older array-family downloads, GSE254052 and GSE11452, exceeded the 2 MB compressed metadata request budget. Only bounded, uninterpreted compressed prefixes were received, and neither was saved or decompressed. These sources are retrieval-incomplete, not rejected through sample-level qualification. Array family SOFT can embed expression tables; subsequent array metadata review uses GEO brief text instead. The yeast compendium's primary paper reports mostly three biological replicates across differing culture conditions, which is insufficient to establish the existing fixed-state bank without a new source contract.

[ART Signature Express](https://maayanlab.cloud/artexpress) provides measured HIV drug and combination signatures across three cell lines and two times on L1000. Its new deposit is GSE314659. A larger, separately scoped brief-metadata request retrieved 944 samples after the original 2 MB request failed. Each of six plates has 20 two-component conditions with exactly dose-matched nominal single labels, alongside higher-order regimens. But those six plates are three different cell lines crossed with two durations; triplicate wells do not establish six independently initiated same-state culture panels. Vehicle fractions remain unqualified because controls are labeled in uM. The [plate-level design audit](../../results/replicated_fixed_state_v1/arts-brief-metadata-20261010-v1/sample_design_and_decision.json) retains this useful lead without accepting it. No ARTS expression files or model weights were opened.

This measured source must not be confused with L1000CDS2's computed combinations of single-drug signatures or DrugComb viability outcomes paired to untreated transcriptomes. Neither substitute supplies observed joint gene-expression effects for this plan.
