# Older GEO and ArrayExpress source search, 10 October 2026

The older GEO query indexed 296 series, including 280 absent from the preceding 296-series inventory. The union contains 576 unique summary records, of which eight additional leads received sample-level review here. Both queries stop at 150 hits under NCBI's default ordering and restrict publication dates through 2024; the inventory is not an exhaustive search of older deposits. The [query configuration](../../configs/datasets/public-discovery-older-20261010.json), pre-request scope and hashed inventory preserve what was actually searched.

The [eight-source decision ledger](../../results/replicated_fixed_state_v1/public-older-followup-20261010-v1/qualification_decisions.json) separates expression comparisons from pooled proliferation screens and mixed chromatin assays. GSE266011 explicitly reports three independent experiments per comparison, but covers two canonical pairs and distinct time strata. GSE243223 has three complete repeats in three cell lines and four in another, rather than six same-state cultures. GSE234689's relevant total-RNA panel has two repeats per arm; its interaction, modification and ribosome libraries do not increase expression replication. None passes the original confirmation gate, and all newly reviewed expression bodies remain unopened.

The search also followed all five ArrayExpress accessions from the [TBX2/MYCN primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6242972/), using bounded BioStudies study JSON and the two relevant SDRF files. E-MTAB-6568 contains 36 unique sources across six knockdown conditions, with six source-reported biological replicates per condition. Its 144 assay rows are four technical records for each source, not 144 cultures. Two TBX2 shRNAs both pair with MYCN, so they supply one canonical gene pair. The source describes sequential transduction and induction; the SDRF lacks an explicit matched independent-initiation block map. This is a useful replicated single-pair lead but cannot support the original unseen-pair experiment. The study API lists raw-sequencing relationships without a processed expression matrix; no FASTQ or expression body was downloaded.

E-MTAB-7025 supplies one THZ1/JQ1 pair at 10 hours with four reported biological repeats and 16 unique sources, represented by 64 technical assay rows. The other three deposits are single-target RNA, ATAC or ChIP experiments. The [SDRF counts and decisions](../../results/replicated_fixed_state_v1/biostudies-neuroblastoma-samples-20261010-v1/qualification_decisions.json) retain these distinctions, including the misleading RNA study-type label on the ATAC source.

The discovery CLI now accepts an explicit bounded query configuration, and the new BioStudies retriever fetches study or SDRF metadata without following expression links. Its sample audit collapses assay rows by source identity, rejects conflicting factors within a source and never infers independent cultures or matched blocks from numbering. Retrieval scopes and hashes remain tracked; raw metadata snapshots remain local because they include contact details. This extends source discovery without changing exposure status or accepting an unqualified biological bank.
