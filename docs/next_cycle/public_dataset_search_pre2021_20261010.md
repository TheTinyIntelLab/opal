# Pre-2021 source search continuation

Explicit publication-date queries through 2020 retrieved 300 GEO series absent from both preceding 296-series inventories, bringing their union to 876 unique summary records. This moves the search beyond the earlier query caps, which had mostly returned newer deposits. Each query still stops at 150 records under NCBI's default ordering, so this is a bounded inventory rather than an exhaustive historical search. The [query configuration](../../configs/datasets/public-discovery-pre2021-20261010.json), scope, translated queries and source hashes preserve its coverage.

Ten additional sources received sample-level review through twenty successful brief-metadata requests. The [historical review](../../results/replicated_fixed_state_v1/public-historical-followup-20261010-v1/qualification_decisions.json) recovered GSE56188, now executed as a [six-repeat unmatched-array diagnostic](lncap_array_unmatched_diagnostic_20261010.md). It also found GSE138256's six nominal yeast drug pairs across changing doses and growth rates, technical replicates in GSE11550, a source dose contradiction in GSE43452, and smaller or differing-state panels in the other historical datasets. These designs cannot simply be pooled into a fixed-state bank.

The [additional review](../../results/replicated_fixed_state_v1/public-pre2021-followup-20261010-v1/qualification_decisions.json) identifies GSE156384 as eighty control iPSC profiles despite its combination-discovery project title. GSE124636 mixes assays, lines and genetic/chemical interventions with three reported repeats, while GSE144353 has three primary donors and one nominal drug pair. The nine sources other than GSE56188 remain expression-unopened in this review. GSE56188's early indexed expression snippets and later scoped complete-matrix access are disclosed separately.

The new diagnostic progresses the empirical workaround without changing the original scientific question. Six source-labeled biological repeats improve the available descriptive evidence, but they do not supply undocumented matched initiation blocks or a broad held-pair graph. The original confirmation and empirical precision requirements remain conditional on a qualified source.
