# Alternatives to the current six-culture study design

The six-culture minimum comes from the current plan's disjoint development/confirmation allocation and its minimum three-culture interval procedure. It is not a universal requirement for all combination-expression research, and six cultures would not guarantee adequate power. The existing protocol remains unchanged: an alternative needs a separate estimand and amendment rather than relabeling a failed gate as passed.

## Recommended immediate route: a retrospective measurement and prediction study

Use available two- or three-repeat sources to characterize technical robustness, systematic response, incremental pair information and predictive performance within those measured datasets. Resolve exact dose, reference and assay metadata before any response preparation. Keep all Combi-seq outcomes explicitly exposed, preserve historical source freezes, and avoid new architecture searches under the original protocol. Additional expression access requires a source-specific scope recorded before loading the matrix.

If a separately amended exploratory model comparison is undertaken, hold every occurrence of each canonical pair out together, nest selection and preprocessing inside training folds, and report results by biological replicate or reported run as documented. Cells, genes, barcodes, technical splits and overlapping folds do not increase the number of independent cultures. Cross-validation can address algorithm selection within a specified procedure; it does not undo prior analyst exposure or create prospective confirmation. The [model-selection literature](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/volume11/cawley10a/cawley10a.pdf) explains why selection must be repeated inside evaluation training splits.

With few independent repeats, present per-repeat losses, model contrasts, exclusions and sensitivity descriptively. Retain uncertainty where a justified procedure exists, but do not manufacture biological confidence intervals from cell-level resampling. The [primary pseudoreplication study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8479118/) demonstrates how ignoring biological replicate structure can produce false discoveries. This route can support a useful comparative or measurement paper, including unfavorable results; it cannot support the original prospective new-culture superiority claim.

## A distinct external-validation design

Develop and select a model on already designated development sources, freeze its transformations, gene universe, hyperparameters and comparisons, and then evaluate a new compatible public study. This can eliminate the need to reserve three development cultures from that new study. It changes the estimand to transfer across the actual source/state/assay differences, with those differences made explicit.

This route requires an unconsumed response bank at the time its test is defined, matched controls/singles, compatible intervention and measurement scales, and sufficient documented independent units for the intended inference. New sources whose expression bodies remain unopened include the sci-Plex-GxE chemical panel and PertSpectra in-house data; neither is yet qualified for this design. Their published narrative results have been encountered and must be disclosed. Two reported repeats still give limited precision; freezing first does not create more cultures, repair unknown initiation or guarantee useful power. Combi-seq and historical exposed sources cannot become fresh confirmation by choosing another seed.

## If the original confirmation claim must remain

Acquire a compatible public source with the required provenance or commission a targeted independent-culture assay. A smaller intervention panel or lower-cost aggregate RNA assay may reduce cost, but the allocation needs empirical power and complete matched references. Reusing existing development measurements could reduce the amount of new data under a justified revised design; collecting only validation replicates is not automatically enough if development and validation differ in assay or state.

Simulated cells, technical partitions, imputed combination responses and borrowed variance assumptions are useful engineering or sensitivity evidence. They cannot replace unmeasured independent biological outcomes. Pooling different tissues, cell lines, time points or studies into nominal replicates changes the population and must not be used to satisfy the original fixed-state contract.

## Work ordering

Proceed with metadata qualification and a retrospective measurement study while continuing targeted source searches. Preserve a separate external-validation proposal until source compatibility and independent units are established. Reserve the original confirmation claim for an accepted empirical design. No primary gate, source exposure history or registration status is changed by this document, and no new expression file or fitted model was consumed while preparing it.
