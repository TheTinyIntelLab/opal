# Data and evaluation research, 20 October 2026

The available evidence favors a finite, noise-aware combination experiment before a larger model. There is enough cached supervision to test a stronger specialized candidate, particularly the 158-pair Wessels expansion, but every existing local pair source is retrospective development. A new split or seed cannot establish independent confirmation.

## What the critical benchmarks actually establish

Ahlmann-Eltze, Huber and Anders compared seven deep models with deliberately simple predictors. Their Norman task supplies all 100 singles and 62 training pairs, then tests 62 other pairs over five partitions; additive prediction wins. Their readout is distance on 1,000 highly expressed genes, rather than Opal's complete retained panel. Their unseen-single experiment finds the strongest linear model uses perturbation embeddings learned from a separate perturbation screen, which supports relevant intervention pretraining over generic atlas size. This does not establish that biological priors are useless. [Published paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-025-02772-6?error=cookies_not_supported&code=0).

The official linear implementation fits training pseudobulks, learns a ten-dimensional basis, and solves regularized matrix regressions with a default penalty of 0.1. External gene and intervention matrices can replace the learned factors. The code uses external local paths and R/Bioconductor dependencies, so the equations are easier to reproduce within Opal than its entire environment. The repository is MIT, with the inspected main commit `bfa6eeea2bd145a1af2ec0127a2e808cc38456a9`. [Implementation](https://github.com/const-ae/linear_perturbation_prediction-Paper/blob/main/benchmark/src/run_linear_pretrained_model.R), [license](https://github.com/const-ae/linear_perturbation_prediction-Paper/blob/main/LICENSE.md).

The newer Miller et al. study, published 1 October 2026, qualifies the earlier conclusion. Across 14 datasets and 18 metrics, it tests whether positive controls can be distinguished from uninformative means. It reports that Norman additivity captures about 88% of the ideal gap on its best-calibrated metrics, while PRESAGE beats additivity on 15 of 18 Wessels metrics. Its Wessels has 28 singles and 157 pairs; training covers 10.3% of the possible pair landscape, compared with 0.63% for its Norman protocol. Its split-cell duplicate measures technical sampling, not independently repeated biology. These findings support response-sensitive evaluation and additional supervision, but do not prove a scaling law or justify replacing unfavorable metrics after seeing results. [Published paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-026-03307-w).

The newer benchmark's official repository separates an MIT core from noncommercial PRESAGE and GPL scLambda components. Full reproduction recommends five GPUs with at least 24 GB each, 384 GB host memory, 64 CPU cores and 2 TB storage. Its preprocessing retains conditions with at least 12 cells and uses library size 10,000, natural-log expression, and 8,192 HVGs plus targets. Its PRESAGE wrapper sums perturbation embeddings for combinations. These choices differ from Opal's thresholds and scale, so published losses cannot be compared numerically to local results. [Official implementation and reproduction instructions](https://github.com/shiftbioscience/Perturbation-Models-Outperform-Baselines).

## scPerturBench is a collection of different prediction tasks

scPerturBench covers 27 methods and 29 datasets. Context generalization predicts known interventions in new contexts; intervention generalization predicts unseen interventions within a context. These tasks cannot be pooled into a single ranking for Opal. The official code is GPL-3.0; its full Podman image is approximately 40 GB and the CPA-only image 12 GB. Reproducing selected methods independently avoids importing a whole heterogeneous benchmark environment. [Official repository](https://github.com/bm2-lab/scPerturBench).

Source inspection of `Perturbation_generalization/Genetic/myGears.py` finds GEARS simulation splits with `train_gene_set_size=.8`, seeds 1, 2 and 3, batch size 32, hidden size 64 and 15 training epochs. The script reads a precomputed 5,000-HVG log-normalized H5AD and relies on external utility modules and absolute paths. Its listed combinatorial datasets include Norman, Wessels, Schmidt and Replogle experiments 6 and 10. This is useful discovery evidence, but each source still needs matching controls/singles, intervention semantics and replication qualification. Inspected repository commit: `698f8ff5ed19530bff171e4205be9b42a46a769d`. [Training script](https://github.com/bm2-lab/scPerturBench/blob/main/Perturbation_generalization/Genetic/myGears.py).

The publisher supplies supplementary methods, dataset tables and source results. The article body is subscription-only in the retrieved page, so this audit does not assert unobserved numerical method rankings. Its public data availability lists original resources as well as Figshare and Zenodo harmonizations. Harmonized availability does not guarantee that a release retains Opal's required singles or metadata. [Publisher record and supplement links](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-025-02980-0?error=cookies_not_supported&code=0).

## The strongest larger public direction has a real extraction cost

Tahoe-100M is CC0. The current official card reports 95,624,334 rows and 337,644,770,670 compressed download bytes. Its fields supply raw sparse counts, cell-line identities, plates and sample identifiers. The first gene/count entry is a marker and must be discarded; controls are `DMSO_TF` and need plate matching. Exact drug concentration comes from sample metadata. Drug-target annotations include GPT-assisted curation, so they should be treated as fallible features. [Official data card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/tahoebio/Tahoe-100M/raw/main/README.md).

A metadata-only API inventory on this audit date shows a 2.29 GB observation table and pseudobulk differential-expression files named as 1,026 shards, with inspected early shards around 80–95 MB. This is not a tiny ready-made mean-response bank. DE statistics also are not interchangeable with mean expression. A bounded new line-by-drug panel could test context transfer with much more contextual diversity, but needs a frozen source revision, metadata-selected subset, actual extraction profiling and matched controls before model fitting. [Official metadata inventory](https://huggingface.co/api/datasets/tahoebio/Tahoe-100M/tree/main/metadata?recursive=true&expand=false).

## Recommended experiment and interpretation

My recommendation is a noise-aware hierarchical residual predictor on the existing observed-single task, with the larger Wessels panel as its main supervision test and Norman as an additional setting. Predict pair departures using training-only low-dimensional structure, allow global and target-level shrinkage, and account for finite-cell uncertainty. This mechanism asks whether noisy small-condition targets have hidden useful shared correction structure. It does not claim that singles identify arbitrary interactions. A generic single-cell backbone has no established advantage at this effective sample size.

Lock grouped pair splits and the complete tuning menu before new scores. Compare additive, global correction, target effects, reduced-rank ridge and selected residual kernels with identical information and fit budgets. Fit bases, gene weights, normalizers and any supervised gene selection inside training partitions. Shared target identities are allowed for the observed-single task, but every occurrence of a held canonical pair must stay out of fitting. A globally unseen-target claim needs a separate split.

Retain full-panel MSE as the primary accuracy guardrail; fix a response-sensitive secondary score from development data and add perturbation-centered retrieval to detect collapse toward a common response. Split-cell positive controls can show whether either metric resolves technical signal, but must be labeled retrospective diagnostics with access to held outcomes. Resample pairs for conditional uncertainty and report target-group sensitivity; neither is independent-culture uncertainty. Evaluate mean uncertainty only unless cell distributions and culture replication actually support stronger claims.

A material gain over the selected simple model on both panels would be PROMISING retrospective evidence. A gain confined to a chosen metric, one small split or a simpler comparator is BASELINE-LEVEL or INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE. If the candidate fails, compare technical reliability with model error before adding capacity. The evidence-backed next change would be reliable additional pair supervision or a deliberately new crossed-context dataset, not another unrestricted architecture search.
