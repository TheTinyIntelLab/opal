Bootstrap intervals from the internal benchmark are descriptive pair and
target-network sensitivities. They do not include full measurement uncertainty

replicate-aware uncertainty, extended-universe training, and complete biological

## Architecture expansion, 5 October 2026

The user authorized implementing and experimentally comparing the architecture research recommendations. The new studies are exploratory, and historical outcomes have informed their design. Their initial endpoint remains equal-pair all-gene response MSE; bounded signal-weighted error is secondary. Both random and overlapping partner holdouts are retained. No candidate is forced to be neural.

The 15-cell Wessels expansion is a separate preparation, preserving exact stratum-support and construct rules. Its first split repartitions the larger identity set, so some reconstructed historical holdout conditions can enter its development set. The study does not read the old evaluator file, but that is not equivalent to withholding those responses from the expanded preparation. It cannot support an independent confirmation claim.

A second `wessels-expanded-protected-log2` track explicitly retains the original forty development and eighteen held-out identities and randomly partitions only newly eligible pairs. It protects historical pair roles for future reproduction. Because the first broad expansion has already been explored, newly reserved pairs are not automatically untouched confirmation data. The report records exposure overlap rather than claiming independence.

Reference uncertainty is a joint technical-cell bootstrap, with one control draw per stratum and one single draw per target reused across pairs. Library normalization, QC and gene eligibility are fixed. Only development doubles are constructed in the architecture-resource adapter. Training losses consume their own training-fold variance rows; validation variance can support scoring after prediction. Technical sampling does not establish biological replication or calibrated predictive intervals.

Public GO/Hallmark annotations and exact symbol mappings form the initial prior bank. Independent Replogle single-screen transfer and author-distributed GenePT/ESM2 priors are a separately recorded extension. External data have their own scale and context; they supply representations and output directions rather than substituting effects across intervention modalities. All public resources are checksum recorded, and external benchmark combination datasets are excluded from extraction.

The neural search compares measured-response, noise, source attention, mean pooling, module outputs, bounded signal loss, shuffled source identities, larger global rank and stronger shrinkage. Independent-transfer studies add encoder/decoder separation and linear loss controls. A prior-kernel extension compares real and shuffled priors under matching searches. The measured-only kernel has fewer candidate configurations, so a win is not a matched-budget efficiency result. Frozen TabICL, GP and training-only retrieval are challengers; their failure is retained. Model selection uses development inner folds, never outer scores or historical evaluator responses on the original tracks.


## Full covariance and retuned-subset extension

After the diagonal-noise architecture studies, new exploratory seeds 20261008, 20261009 and 20261010 compare 67 unweighted, 201 diagonal and 469 correlated candidates. A 469-candidate training-covariance permutation controls the correlated search budget. Covariance uses only joint technical cell-bootstrap residuals and training blocks, with the historical fixed gene/QC/normalizer schema. Sixteen refits condition on chosen configurations and covariance; no biological or model-selection uncertainty is claimed.

Learning curves retune each subset, preserving infeasible small subsets rather than redrawing them. `retuned-curves-v1` reordered even the full-size pairs and hence changed its inner partition. `retuned-curves-v2` restores canonical order at full size, verifies equality with the frozen full-size unweighted comparator and retains v1. Fitting-only tuning precedes untouched calibration-label quantiles. Requested finite ranks above calibration sample counts are unavailable, not clipped; pair dependence prevents guaranteed coverage claims. No new historical query response access is authorized or performed.

## Context-transfer amendment, 6 October 2026

The historical same-context evaluator is unchanged. New chemical source adapters
normalize counts to 10,000, transform log2 per cell, then average; the historical
track transforms after condition averaging. Exact intervention, dose and endpoint
identities determine single/pair matching. Missing storage zeros carry false
availability flags and never stand in for observed zero effects. Fitting,
group-max calibration, query identities and evaluator responses live in distinct
artifacts. Six residual families are selected using source contexts only.

EmeraldBay is an exposed exploratory pilot with 54 fitting, 18 calibration and
27 evaluation rows. The D-SPIN independent study freezes EmeraldBay's pooled-sum
procedure (alpha 0.01, bandwidth 4), then refits on source D-SPIN contexts; it
does not transfer fitted weights across assays. Myeloid and NK are withheld
across both batches, and inner selection holds out entire lineages. The frozen
procedure fails the registered 5% improvement gate; no later holdout tuning
replaces that result. A source-calibrated empirical radius has no out-of-context
coverage guarantee, as the observed 34.3% coverage demonstrates.

Estimated singles use a separate schema and mask all query singles from fitting
and imputation references. Exact intervention sources are disclosed and absent
sources trigger abstention. Its reported total, main, residual and cross-term
errors expose cancellation. Native CPA and GEARS retain upstream architecture
and loss while adapting aggregate observations; GEARS graphs derive only from
allowed singles. These are not canonical cell-level comparisons. Acquisition
policies share fixed validation sets and dependent seeds, so their results do not
establish a universal prospective policy. New runs use new directories and
retain protocol/source fingerprints; cycle-v6's disk-full failure is preserved.
