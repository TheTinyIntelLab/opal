## Source and protocol boundaries

- Norman uses the count-preserving scPerturb v1.1 source; Wessels uses its original GEX/GDO archive and matched THP1 metadata. Exact source hashes and count locations are in `data/source_registry.json`, preparation manifests, and pinned dataset configs. This isn't the GEARS compatibility preprocessing track.
- Genetic effects transform normalized condition means. The chemical/context adapters transform each normalized cell before averaging. Scores on these scales aren't interchangeable. Technical lanes, cells, and batches aren't assumed independent biological replicates.
- The broad Wessels expansion exposed historical query identities. The protected expansion preserves their old roles but isn't independent confirmation, because some new query identities were already explored. The exposure counts and amendments remain in recorded results and the manuscript.
- Primary selections are preserved. Later kernel, external-prior, noise, capacity, and state studies are exploratory amendments informed by historical results. Repeated folds are dependent; technical uncertainty excludes biological and selection uncertainty.
- Historical evaluator fingerprints weren't all frozen before fitting. Retrospective source/prediction/order audits don't imply a historical freeze. New execution guards reject changed or reordered evaluators before fitting. Corrected retuned curves preserve the earlier partition-changing version.
- D-SPIN holds entire Myeloid and NK lineages across batches, uses source-only selection and separate calibration, and explicitly permits measured held-context singles for normalization. Its reference is a pooled-pair kernel; the frozen pooled-sum procedure fails the registered superiority gate. This isn't zero-shot weight transfer.
- Native CPA/GEARS retain upstream architecture and loss with aggregate-input adaptations and disclosed information access. PertAdapt feasibility fails on recorded memory/runtime constraints; this says nothing about its biological inferiority. Unfavorable scores and interrupted runs remain in `results/`.

Pinned external source-inspection files are retained under `data/source_audits/architecture-20261005/`, with their original license and source manifest. Historical manifests may refer to the earlier `docs/research_sources/` path; those records aren't rewritten as new experiments. See `paper/main.pdf` for complete methods, results, citations, and limitations, and `results/state/capability_ledger.json` for the development capability ledger.
