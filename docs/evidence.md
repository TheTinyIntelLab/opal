# Scientific evidence

Start with [research findings](research.md), which links the six-task growth ledger, failed external transfer and negative experiments. The [archive index](archive/README.md) groups original protocols, source records, measurement audits and historical reports without changing their bytes.

Results under `results/` are compact scientific evidence, not pretrained model downloads. Hashes establish byte identity, not biological independence or legal permission. Dated test counts and builds apply only to the revisions identified in their receipts. The [v1 checklist](../RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md) governs this release's validation status.
