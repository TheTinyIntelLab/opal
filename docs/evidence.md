Pretreatment controls, numeric doses, pair products and mixed kernels do not earn advancement. One-hot identity renaming is invariant, so an explicitly registered row-permutation amendment supplies the destructive control without replacing the original result. Dose shuffling never establishes chemical identity information. Baseline-only pair ratios against observed-single competitors are excluded from rankings because their information contracts differ.

The neural audit retains both original 80-update controls and searches 16 width/rank/rate/decay configurations per shared/pair family, two seeds and 120 updates. Wessels selected pair members have training MSE about 0.00022 to 0.00024 versus inner ensemble MSE 0.0083 to 0.0098, favoring a generalization problem over a capacity shortage. Some pair members reach the epoch cap, so longer-run convergence remains untested. Norman has a smaller training/validation gap but still loses to simple correction. These diagnostics do not justify widening the grid after outer evaluation.

Cross-reference technical halves preserve whole shared-single/control matrices, and saved accounting verifies `total = main + residual + cross term`. Their residual direction agreement remains weak, and component cancellation is substantial. Frozen QC, normalizers and panels prevent treating these as independent culture estimates or biological interaction recovery. Culture precision and a reliable pair-specific biological signal remain unevaluable.

Evidence is under [`results/stage1-20261007/report-v2/`](../results/stage1-20261007/report-v2/): full comparisons, decision, measurement diagnostics, neural diagnosis and claim-specific OOD isolation manifest. Fold predictions, every candidate trajectory, selections, exclusions and source fingerprints remain in `run-v1/`, `main-v1/` and `identity-control-v1/`. The studies used 631.67 aggregate seconds and at most 1.06 GB process working set; local export profiling is separate. Interrupted `report-v1`, float32 replay `exports-v1` and corrected float64 `exports-v2` are all preserved.

The next justified step is source qualification for a crossed screen with exact singles and controls, independently initiated cultures, globally held pair identities and multiple sealed pretreatment states. The exposed EmeraldBay joint-isolation manifest verifies exclusions, with sparse query coverage; it is not a confirmation experiment. Repeating D-SPIN cannot supply new confirmation. Chemical identity descriptors need separately licensed, provenance-checked mappings. Reliability, acquisition and scaling remain conditional.

Opal is a family of symmetric output-level residual predictors with a benchmark pipeline. With both singles measured in the same biological setting, it predicts their sum plus a fitted correction. Improved total-response error doesn't by itself establish pair-specific biological interaction learning.

Raw data and ignored prediction arrays aren't bundled. Full reproduction requires source downloads, optional dependencies, and sometimes the recorded GPU environment. Interrupted and corrected runs are retained.

Software tests cover split isolation, exports, symmetry, and restoration. Release verification runs the installed wheel outside the checkout and replays the released references. ## Verification and remaining gaps

The revised source passes 117 tests. A 0.1.1 wheel installed outside the checkout reproduces 38 saved Norman and 27 EmeraldBay predictions with maximum errors of 2.03e-15 and 1.33e-15. The package dependencies were reused from the host; this wasn't a fresh installation of the dependency closure. The synthetic CLI and pair-order checks pass. The 27-page manuscript and extracted TeX sources compile locally; arXiv server compilation and moderation are untested.

The original full roadmap isn't complete. Hosting was explicitly moved to separate projects. A PertAdapt biological score, extended-output training, fuller biological-replicate analysis, canonical cell-level native comparisons, and prospective experiment-selection validation remain unestablished. Failed transfer and encoder gates are completed experiments, not missing positive results.

## Source and protocol boundaries

- Norman uses the count-preserving scPerturb v1.1 source; Wessels uses its original GEX/GDO archive and matched THP1 metadata. Exact source hashes and count locations are in `data/source_registry.json`, preparation manifests, and pinned dataset configs. This isn't the GEARS compatibility preprocessing track.
- Genetic effects transform normalized condition means. The chemical/context adapters transform each normalized cell before averaging. Scores on these scales aren't interchangeable. Technical lanes, cells, and batches aren't assumed independent biological replicates.
- The broad Wessels expansion exposed historical query identities. The protected expansion preserves their old roles but isn't independent confirmation, because some new query identities were already explored. The exposure counts and amendments remain in recorded results and the manuscript.
- Primary selections are preserved. Later kernel, external-prior, noise, capacity, and state studies are exploratory amendments informed by historical results. Repeated folds are dependent; technical uncertainty excludes biological and selection uncertainty.
- Historical evaluator fingerprints weren't all frozen before fitting. Retrospective source/prediction/order audits don't imply a historical freeze. New execution guards reject changed or reordered evaluators before fitting. Corrected retuned curves preserve the earlier partition-changing version.
- D-SPIN holds entire Myeloid and NK lineages across batches, uses source-only selection and separate calibration, and explicitly permits measured held-context singles for normalization. Its reference is a pooled-pair kernel; the frozen pooled-sum procedure fails the registered superiority gate. This isn't zero-shot weight transfer.
- Native CPA/GEARS retain upstream architecture and loss with aggregate-input adaptations and disclosed information access. PertAdapt feasibility fails on recorded memory/runtime constraints; this says nothing about its biological inferiority. Unfavorable scores and interrupted runs remain in `results/`.

Pinned external source-inspection files are retained under `data/source_audits/architecture-20261005/`, with their original license and source manifest. Historical manifests may refer to the earlier `docs/research_sources/` path; those records aren't rewritten as new experiments. See `paper/main.pdf` for complete methods, results, citations, and limitations, and `results/state/capability_ledger.json` for the development capability ledger.
