## Finite proposed roster

- B0 is zero, TRAIN mean, measured-single sum and measured-single minimum. Sum/minimum operate in the declared endpoint units, not standardized singles.
- B1 is intercept plus single sum, absolute difference and product, penalties 1, 10, 100, 1000. B2 adds gene-incidence columns with the same four penalties. All gene effects come only from local TRAIN labels.
- B3 uses 64 fixed radial-basis columns over sorted standardized single coordinates, with TRAIN-pair centers selected by stable hash prefix and bandwidth equal to median nonzero TRAIN coordinate distance, floored at 1e-8. Include B1 columns; fit with and without gene-incidence columns at penalties 10 and 100. This is four configurations. Do not tune centers, bandwidth or count after evaluation.
- I0 retains one source-local rank-eight residual completion recipe, signed symmetric rank with four positive and four negative directions, penalty 0.001 and 500 Adam steps at learning rate 0.03. This is a NEW one-context comparator if the historical adapter requires two contexts; never pass duplicated contexts and call it native historical reuse. Root must bind its exact equation, loss normalization and checkpoint rule before execution. Fixed 500th step is preferred to uncharged early stopping.
- I1 is the symmetric tensor pair kernel specified below, 64 Nyström landmarks and ridge penalties 10 and 100. It has no learned gene embeddings.
- I2 is a small NAIAD-like residual composition, with gene embedding dimension 4 and a shared linear 4-to-8-to-4 ReLU transform. Sum the two transformed embeddings, concatenate their elementwise product, then apply an 8-to-16-to-1 ReLU residual head. Use fixed 300 full-batch Adam steps at 0.01, float64, embedding initialization N(0,0.05), hidden biases zero, deterministic Xavier weights, no dropout, L2 mean-square parameter penalties 0.001 and 0.01. Optimize mean residual MSE plus that penalty and take step 300. This is two configurations, not a reproduction of official NAIAD or its active-learning algorithm.

One frozen fit seed per cell/budget is enough; data seeds are not optimization-seed candidates. No extra graph, pretrained, ensemble, width, rank, restart, epoch or feature search enters the primary roster. Approximate basis sizes are min(64, observed TRAIN pair count).

## A pair kernel that is distinct from flexible singles

For genes i,u define k(i,u)=0.5*1[i=u]+0.5*exp(-(z_i-z_u)^2/2), using standardized measured singles z. For unordered pairs define K({i,j},{u,v}) = 0.5*[k(i,u)k(j,v)+k(i,v)k(j,u)]. This is a positive semidefinite symmetrized tensor kernel and is invariant to either pair's orientation. Its identity component and mixed identity/single components let sharing a particular gene change similarity even when two genes have identical single effects. B3 is a smooth function of the two single values plus additive gene effects; it cannot generally represent gene-specific partner-response curves. I1 has such curves without free latent factor optimization. This is an architectural distinction, not additional information, because every family already receives gene identities and singles.

Choose landmarks by the same stable hash order on observed TRAIN pairs, without labels. Let W be the landmark Gram matrix. Compute eigenvectors and retain eigenvalues greater than 1e-10, then Phi=K(query,landmarks)*U*diag(eigenvalue^-0.5). Ridge-fit Phi on TRAIN residuals and store landmark identities, eigenbasis and coefficients. No TEST-based bandwidth or landmarks. If all eigenvalues are removed, return the shared m and record the degeneracy. A unit test with identical singles but different shared-gene identities must distinguish I1 from a singles-only RBF; symmetry and PSD tests must pass before freeze.

This kernel remains limited: without qualified external features it cannot predict arbitrary low-rank latent similarity between entirely distinct genes. That failure is informative, not a reason to add oracle factors. The structured-nonlinear generator must state whether its signal uses measured singles, identities or additional available inputs. If it uses a feature absent from every model, label it an unrecoverable-input control rather than an architecture target.
