## Questions and gaps

Does baseline expression predict an exact intervention's response beyond its
source-context average? Existing evidence does not establish that. A new-context
baseline input route, fold-scoped preprocessing, compact learned state model,
and controlled staged/joint comparisons are missing. Query singles in the
observed-single task cannot answer this main-effect question.

There is no qualified replicated crossed-context genetic combination corpus,
positive independent confirmation, validated shift-risk policy, time-resolved
operator, or higher-order confirmation. Public method adaptations have narrower
contracts than their canonical cell-level implementations.

Keep all exposed, negative and interrupted runs, including the disk-full cycle.
All new artifacts use new directories. Initial free space was approximately
8.45 GB on C and 3.16 GB on D. A new 2.53 GB count source fits on C; preparation
will stream sparse batches, retain compact condition means and require at least
3.5 GB free before downloading. No prior evidence is removed. Downloads, fitted
arrays, training state and clean-install environments remain outside Git.

New biological results are development unless an exposure-reviewed frozen
confirmation is explicitly registered. Existing D-SPIN outcomes will not be
reopened as fresh confirmation.
