## Frozen population and identity

Bind the phenotype gzip, guide-library gzip and historical profiles NPZ to the three hashes in the executable runner. Verify literal ordered guide names against unique 20nt sequences in the metadata before converting the four selected response columns. K562 columns 13/14 and Jurkat columns 4/5 must have the exact context, Triple Sequencing and Replicate 1/2 headers. Those infections are separate measurements within the source, not independent source replication. Reject duplicate ordered constructs, unmatched names, truncated rows, duplicate guide names and sequences. Non-finite or unparsable deposited measurement fields are unavailable, never zero.

For each exact guide, arm, context and infection, compute its single as the equal mean of finite target/non-targeting constructs in the same arm against at least two distinct opposite-arm controls. Double-target and double-control rows never contribute to singles. Distinct controls follow from duplicate construct rejection. Metadata has no context itself; context and infection are read only from the qualified headers. Preserve guide names, sequences, explicit missing annotations and support counts. All controls remain dependent shared assay measurements.

Stream again for doubles. Exclude controls and self-gene pairs, retain the exact historical 81,466 canonical distinct-gene pairs without silently dropping any pair. Retain a construct only when its gamma and both arm-matched singles are finite in that context/infection. Require two ordered constructs per pair per infection for a qualified aggregate; require both infections for a context-level diagnostic panel. Preserve full-roster counts, raw finite counts, missing-single counts, qualification masks, orientation counts and means, with NaN for unavailable qualified aggregates. Report coverage and exclusion counts on the complete roster and unchanged union of five Q2 block tests. Never reassign a guide or orientation to another pair split.

## Predictions, metrics and gates

On the identical matched constructs, average gamma and predictions equally per canonical pair/infection, then equally over infections. Compute zero, construct-first sum/minimum, mean-first sum/minimum, and historical gene-single sum/minimum on this same matched target and pair panel. The historical predictor uses its original measurements and weights and is a separate information representation, not an aggregation-order control. Report each panel's MSE, MAE, bias, Pearson and target variance, matched-versus-historical target MSE, nonlinear minimum prediction disagreement and availability of both orientations. Report all-qualified and qualified original-block-test panels separately; only the latter receives the fixed 2,000-draw paired endpoint-multiplicity bootstrap, seed 20261022, for exact-guide minimum loss minus historical minimum loss. Bounds are conditional gene sensitivity, not biological-replicate or independent-source confidence.

The scientific integrity gate requires matched sum aggregation to commute to absolute tolerance 1e-12 in each context with at least one evaluable cell. Header, mapping, duplicate and frozen-source checks must pass. Numerical arrays and JSON results must replay exactly, including missingness. This integrity plus successful replay is the prerequisite for Q2b's unchanged original scalar-bank flexible-function test. Q2b does not consume this changed reagent target or fitted guide data.

A stronger, separate guide-aware historical-comparison gate requires all 81,466 pairs eligible in both infections, no finite constructs lost to missing singles, and reconstructed target differences at most 1e-12 versus each historical infection target. Failure stops guide-aware historical refitting or claims on the original population. It does not stop Q2b on the unchanged six original scalar banks. No loss ranking promotes a model or changes eligibility. No biological interaction conclusion follows from this no-fit diagnostic, irrespective of the minimum ordering result.
