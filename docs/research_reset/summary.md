# Opal research reset, 20 October 2026

Retain completion as an original-screen baseline and narrow the objective to assay-conditioned functional prediction with measured singles and an explicit local pair-training budget. Some residual predictive information survives infection changes within the original study, but the unchanged learned correction does not reliably transfer across studies, even when local singles replace its reference. The [root plan](../../plan.md) defines the next gates; no new predictor is promoted.

## Decisive findings

The historical external result is valid within its contract. Frozen completion has 1.08-2.15% higher MSE than frozen main effects across four cohorts from two studies, and original-single minimum wins every cohort. Conditional gene intervals cross zero, so this is failed superiority rather than established universal harm. All 22 frozen input hashes still match, and source headers, canonical identities and row alignment check out.

The independent audit changes the interpretation of novelty and coverage. Across those cohorts there are only 509 unique bank-novel pairs, and all appear in the original raw source but fail joint-context coverage eligibility. Their genes occupy a sparse block; only four original test pairs connect two genes in their union. The random-pair test barely identifies the external missing-block task. Ordinary low-degree source pairs retain gains, so degree alone does not explain the external loss.

## Experiments run

Independent agents audited evidence, reviewed current primary literature and ran cross-infection development concurrently. Separately frozen support and reference-swap branches extended the diagnosis. Every reused source is explicitly exploratory.

The numerical branches used tests and frozen hashes before their relevant comparisons. The first assay branch also had a pre-result registration commit, `b7aff5e`. Development used four neural fits, without a rank/seed/penalty search, and took 9.77 seconds. Four checkpoint predictions replay exactly across 16,294 pairs. Historical installed prediction replays permit numerical roundoff up to 1e-12. Reports disclose conditional uncertainty, incomplete fit-memory instrumentation and narrower replay checks rather than claiming biological confidence or complete resource enforcement.

## Direction and alternatives

The next decisive development test holds entire pair blocks out of training and compares stronger calibrated-single/node policies, existing completion and native NAIAD with identical information. NAIAD directly addresses scalar combination phenotypes; GEARS, CPA and TxPert are relevant prior art for their expression tasks, but growth heads would be adaptations. No native competitor reproduction or structured-model superiority is claimed here.

SPIDR responses remain unopened. Its library metadata was acquired and projected through a tested allowlist that discards historical guide-activity annotations without numerical conversion or use. It is RPE-1 TP53 KO, uses deliberate guide mismatches and has an LFC endpoint, with cultures split after transduction. A later locally supervised learning-procedure test on it would be independent-source replication after adaptation, not zero-shot original-weight transfer or independently initiated biological replication.

The plan retains chemical growth and better-replicated expression as contingent endpoint alternatives. It requires robust gains over strong references before increasing architecture complexity. If those gains fail, retaining calibration and publishing a reproducible negative benchmark is preferable to a larger model without identified signal.
