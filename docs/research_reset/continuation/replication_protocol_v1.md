# Synthetic replication and predictability boundaries, v1

Prospective registration after metadata discovery, before generating any outcomes or fitting. This is an engineering and experimental-design study, not biological validation or a continuation of the stopped strongest-reference experiment. No biological files are read. No architecture search or retry follows a negative result.

Question: when does assay-local completion predict a separately initiated measurement, and can prediction distinguish a persistent technical pair effect from a biological interaction? Wave Five established low-rank recovery and observational aliasing in one assay. This study adds an explicit second initiation, competing references, correlated construct noise and unpredictable edge effects.

Freeze the complete factorial menu of five worlds, seeds 6101, 6102, 6103 and construct-noise standard deviations 0 and 0.1, giving 30 cells. Use 60 genes and all 1,770 unordered pairs, assigned once per seed by a seeded permutation to 885 TRAIN, 354 VALIDATION and 531 TEST pairs. Every gene must have TRAIN support or the entire registered run stops. The same assignment applies to both initiations. Singles are independently measured synthetic gene effects, normal with standard deviation 0.2, plus independent single-measurement noise with standard deviation noise/2. All methods receive identical measured singles and TRAIN pair targets.

The worlds are additive only; persistent signed rank-two biological signal; identically persistent signed rank-two technical signal; signed rank-two technical signal independently redrawn for initiation two; and independent sparse pair effects persistent across initiations. Generate signed rank-two signal as the difference of two independent normal factor products, normalized to pair RMS 0.3. Sparse effects use independent Bernoulli probability 0.1 and uniform negative magnitudes between 0.2 and 0.6, then RMS 0.3. No favorable pairs or seeds are selected. Biological and persistent-technical worlds use exactly the same observed arrays, with different causal labels, so any apparent identification is falsified by construction.

Each initiation has four construct measurements per pair. Noise equals noise*sqrt(0.5) times one shared pair normal plus noise*sqrt(0.5) times a construct-specific normal. The pair mean has variance noise^2*(0.5+0.5/4); these constructs are dependent pseudo-replicates, not four initiations. Singles, factors, assignments and errors are generated in a fixed stream schedule independent of world labels. No uncertainty claim treats seeds, pairs or constructs as biological replicates.

Reference candidates are the unchanged ScalarReference grid: zero, mean, additive, minimum, and calibration/node ridge at alpha 1, 10, 100, 1000. Select by initiation-one VALIDATION MSE, breaking ties by menu order. Completion fits that selected reference's TRAIN residual with unchanged ScalarCompletion, rank 2, penalty 0.0001, 400 steps, seed equal to the cell seed and scale=max(TRAIN target standard deviation, 1e-6). Its checkpoint step uses only initiation-one VALIDATION residuals. A second completion receives one fixed seeded shuffle of TRAIN residuals. No hyperparameter selection across cells.

Bind code and environment before execution. Save all 30 cells' input hashes, selected references, checkpoint hashes and both TEST prediction vectors before any TEST scoring. Then report every cell's same-initiation and second-initiation observed and noiseless-target MSE for selected reference, aligned completion and shuffled completion. Also report the true conditional-mean oracle. Serialization must replay exactly. No biological significance test or promotion is performed. A descriptive synthetic recovery claim requires at least 5% lower pooled second-initiation MSE than the selected reference in all three persistent-biological noisy seeds, exact replay, and the observationally identical technical world must explicitly receive the same prediction evidence. Failure is preserved without changing thresholds.

Resource cap: one process, one BLAS/Torch thread, 600 wall seconds, 2 GiB process-tree peak memory, 10 MiB new run artifacts and the unchanged 1 GiB free-disk reserve. Serialize fits. Abort on nonfinite data, input/code drift, missing TRAIN support, replay mismatch or resource-cap breach. Operational launch failure may be corrected with a separately preserved receipt; scientific cells may not be tuned or rerun to obtain success. Discovery inputs inform only the prospective design; no third-party simulator is executed or vendored.
