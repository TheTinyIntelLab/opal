## What the nuisance audit can establish

The exact sidecar has one active control-partner bitset, 196606, representing the same sixteen controls in all supported guide/arm/context/infection cells. Control identity composition therefore has no distinguishing variation in this panel. Two metadata controls remain absent from the deposited source, with cause unknown. Construct counts vary from four to eighteen; orientation imbalance is exactly zero, and mean signed arm-shift sum cancels to numerical precision. These constant covariates were retained under the frozen recipe rather than removed after looking at results. Measurable arm differences, guide-derived second moments and dispersion were calibrated, but this finite linear feature family does not exhaust technical effects.

Under a fixed guide library, stable guide/arm effects can be confounded with gene effects. More fundamentally, a technical effect attached to a canonical gene pair and repeated in its constructs or infections enters the same observed target as a biological interaction. Held-pair accuracy, infection agreement, gene resampling and coarse residual shuffling do not provide an independent measurement that separates those components. Completion can predict pair-aligned structure beyond these nuisance summaries without identifying its biological origin. All Horlbeck and previous cohorts are exposed development data; neither context is independent-source confirmation.
