# Literature and source audit after external failure

Audit date: 20 October 2026. This is an evidence record, not a second forward plan. `plan.md` owns decisions, budgets and execution order. No model was fitted by this investigation. The SPIDR library ZIP was acquired and its identity/sequence metadata projected. That library contains historical guide-activity annotations, whose field values were discarded without numerical conversion or scoring. No SPIDR response/count table was downloaded or opened.

The strongest immediate objective is a narrow assay-conditioned growth capability whose pair-specific improvement survives strong single-effect predictors and independent measurements. The external loss does not justify a larger expression model. It does justify testing whether assay-matched singles restore useful prediction, whether the residual completion term transfers after that restoration, and whether its apparent within-screen gain reproduces across measurements. These are different hypotheses and need separate controls.

## Repository evidence and exposure

The inspection read the current root plan, README, `docs/model_development_20261020.md`, the architecture/data reviews dated 20 October, the frontier reviews, source/method registries, the frozen external contract and the external numeric results. The external contract explicitly forbids fitting, replacement singles, fitted scaling and intercepts. Its four cohorts come from two studies with shared laboratory lineage. Their outcomes, the original Horlbeck split, the Norman/Wessels development outcomes and earlier chemical/context diagnostics are exposed. Any successor scored on them is development or exploratory evidence, even when a new split or bootstrap seed is used.

The original external result is consistent with substantial assay/reagent shift because local measured-single predictions are much better than frozen original-single predictions. It does not isolate shift as the cause: context, reagents, growth windows, depletion censoring and selection into gene coverage change together. Small positive completion-minus-main-effect loss differences with intervals spanning zero establish failed superiority under the registered gate, not a statistically established universal inferiority or absence of biological interactions. The reported four cohorts are correlated because they reuse pair identities and study procedures.

The bounded source-exposure search used current `docs`, registry/acquisition records and filenames under `data`/`results`, followed by:

```powershell
git log --all --oneline -G 'PRJNA988447|PMC12018271|s41586-025-08815|SPIDR|PRJNA701256|SCHEMATIC' -- docs data configs tools results
```

There were no matching history entries or pre-existing current-file records for these two new leads. That supports their status as unscored in the available repository record; it cannot certify that no human has previously read their publications. This audit inspected study narratives and metadata. Those exposures must be recorded when registering future evaluation.

The previously inspected pins are recorded in `docs/research_20261020_architectures.md` and `data/public_method_registry.json`; this review does not claim to have rerun those implementations. Code availability does not establish that all graphs, weights and preprocessing assets are redistributable. In particular, the TxPert README identifies its EULA, but this investigation did not newly parse the full license PDF or grant commercial clearance.

Two benchmark results need to coexist. Ahlmann-Eltze, Huber and Anders found deliberately simple predictors competitive with seven deep methods; their Norman double task supplies observed constituent singles and holds complete double conditions. Systema shows why mean-like predictions can look strong when shared perturbation effects dominate standard metrics. These support mean, no-change and calibrated-single controls rather than proving that every expressive model must fail. [Critical benchmark](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-025-02772-6), [Systema paper and official framework](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-025-02777-8).

Miller et al., published 1 October 2026, find that metric calibration can reveal advantages hidden by uninformative summary metrics and report favorable Wessels results for PRESAGE. Its normalization, population and metrics differ from Opal. This is evidence for an independently specified response-sensitive secondary evaluation, not permission to replace a failed primary metric retrospectively. [Published study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-026-03307-w), [official benchmark](https://github.com/shiftbioscience/Perturbation-Models-Outperform-Baselines).

## A genuinely unscored source candidate

Fielden et al., *Comprehensive interrogation of synthetic lethality in the DNA damage response*, Nature 2025, is the highest-priority new source lead. SPIDR measures approximately 150,000 DDR gene pairs with CRISPRi in hTERT RPE-1 TP53-knockout cells. Its two screen cultures were divided after transduction during selection, so they are not independent infection initiations. The source is from a different laboratory, but shares guide-design lineage with earlier CRISPRi resources and deliberately includes mismatched guides. Its endpoint is T14/T0 log-fold depletion rather than Horlbeck's per-doubling gamma. Article and supplement rights are CC BY 4.0 unless individually excepted; raw SRA rights were not separately verified. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08815-4), [primary full-text XML with methods and license](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC12018271/fullTextXML), [author-institution license record](https://doi.org/10.3929/ethz-b-000731794), [raw project PRJNA988447](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/bioproject/PRJNA988447).

The following publisher routes were verified by HEAD requests. A subsequent metadata-only qualification acquired Supplementary Table 2; Supplementary Table 8 remains unopened. The named table numbers follow the primary article, not an inferred filename order.

The `/full/` CDN path returned HTTP 200 with a 3,038-byte HTML document; the `/original/` paths above returned the expected binary content types and lengths. A downloader must verify content signatures and hashes rather than trust HTTP status. The author's interactive SPIDR site rendered only an app shell here, so it is not the acquisition route. Published globally inferred GEMINI scores must not become supervised prediction targets or priors because their calculation uses the full pair bank.

This remains a candidate for future confirmation, not a qualified zero-shot gamma benchmark. The completed metadata qualification found 41 of 427 original supported genes and all 820 possible distinct pairs among those 41 genes. Of those pairs, 684 occur in the original eligible bank and 136 do not. Only 21 supported genes have an exact original-guide sequence match; the corresponding 210 possible gene pairs are a metadata bound, not verified matched-construct coverage or observed support. These counts make source-level transfer testable on a narrow panel, but do not establish endpoint compatibility, power or independent culture replication. See `spidr_metadata.md` for the reproducible qualification and acquisition boundary.

Fix the eligible gene/guide panel, exact endpoint, split and prediction assets before an evaluator opens the count table. If per-doubling scaling is unavailable, use a separately registered LFC task rather than estimating scale from held outcomes. A target-single-informed successor may use target singles by declared design, but cannot fit target-pair calibration outside a separately counted adaptation partition.

The paper's preparation discards strong perfectly matched guides using observed depletion. Copying that filter would introduce outcome-dependent eligibility. Keep every prespecified guide variant separately or choose a sequence-only rule; T0 coverage filtering can be used without screening on T14 response. Independently score perfect and mismatched guide strata. Same-gene/perfect/mismatched constructs cannot be counted as independent pair identities or silently averaged into an unchanged perturbation dose. A future confirmatory claim must state that source/lab transfer and new-context transfer are confounded here, and that split cultures do not provide new independent infections.

SCHEMATIC is a second independent source candidate if SPIDR fails metadata qualification. Fong et al. describe a 67-by-176 gene library across seven adherent cell models, duplicate screens and four times. Supplementary Data 1 provides read counts and Supplementary Data 2 contains interaction scores; raw data are PRJNA701256. This changes CRISPRi to nuclease knockout and therefore needs a distinct task. Its manuscript/deposit license and exact replicate-initiation map remain unqualified. The [later metadata/code review](spidr_qualification_next.md) finds that published partner-median singles incorporate double-perturbation outcomes and cannot be used as independent inputs; direct target/non-targeting controls need a separately registered evaluator. Do not treat derived cross-context synthetic-essentiality calls as label-independent inputs. [Primary study and data availability](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12266356/), [publisher](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-024-01971-9).

## Alternative endpoints and evidence that would change the decision

NCI-ALMANAC is already locally acquired as a fallback, but the current record says no model or endpoint search ran. Its drug-pair growth task provides a route to dense dose/context prediction, with exact concentration, laboratory/site and assay identity preserved. It is a new chemical task, not external confirmation of genetic gamma. First audit the locally retained archive and original provenance, then declare a simple HSA/minimum-single, Bliss where its measurement scale permits it, dose-response and context-specific baseline contract. Never mix drug-synergy definitions as though they were the same biological label. [NCI project description](https://www.cancer.gov/news-events/cancer-currents-blog/2017/nci-almanac-drug-combinations), [NCI CellMiner access](https://discover.nci.nih.gov/cellminer/html/drug_almanac_combo_score.html).

Expression remains an alternative if a new task offers matched singles, enough distinct pairs, honest culture replication and a fixed biological endpoint. The existing Norman/Wessels/TNF/Combi-seq results are already development evidence. Large single-intervention corpora can test context or intervention representation, but their scale does not identify arbitrary double interactions. Cell-distribution modeling requires cell-level targets and distribution-sensitive evaluation; a successful mean predictor would not establish distributional fidelity.

Three cheap information tests should precede new capacity. First, compare replicate-specific single-calibrated residuals using independent single estimates where possible, because a noisy shared single enters both pair features and residual labels and can create apparent antagonism. Second, permute training residuals while preserving gene degree and observation-count structure, then refit the same completion operator; loss of gain tests whether pair assignments contain predictive information beyond the baseline. Third, perform a frozen residual-transfer diagnostic with local measured singles, comparing an identical zero-residual predictor and a fixed original completion correction. Improvement of the local-single baseline alone supports assay-specific main effects; improvement of the correction as well supports residual transfer. Either result is exploratory on the four exposed cohorts.

Report these tests on fixed whole-pair partitions with paired losses, equal pair weighting and separate observation-count/guide strata. Pair and gene resampling quantify different conditional uncertainties; neither manufactures independently initiated cultures. Small conditional MSE gains justify further work only when they exceed the registered practical margin and survive the stronger singles reference. A frontier claim would additionally need independent source/culture replication, competitive native scalar baselines such as NAIAD, useful prospective decisions and externally replayable artifacts. Reimplementing GEARS, CPA, TxPert or a low-rank operator alone supplies none of that evidence.

## Limits of this investigation

Primary papers, official repositories, source metadata, public article XML and metadata-only HEAD responses were inspected. The later library-only ZIP projection is recorded separately and discards historical guide-activity field values. Some Nature and PMC HTML requests returned interstitials; the primary EuropePMC XML supplied the SPIDR methods and license without reading response tables. No complete native competitor was reproduced, no novel source passed empirical response qualification, and no biological replication beyond the published initiation descriptions was inferred. Proposed residual tests remain recommendations in this literature audit; the root plan and executable protocol determine what actually runs.
