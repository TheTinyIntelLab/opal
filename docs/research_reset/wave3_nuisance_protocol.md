## Question and identifiable scope

Does fixed rank-eight completion retain the historical 5% gain after validation-selected simple calibration of defensibly measurable guide/arm nuisance? Retain existing completion and calibrated main-effect predictions as historical references. Also fit a new completion residual on the selected guide-aware simple baseline so its baseline, available singles, pair-label information and test pairs are identical to its comparator. This second comparison is the primary equal-information discriminator. The historical completion has a coarser single representation and is a secondary frozen comparator.

Read and bind Q1b prepared arrays and exact replay, its control-partner identity sidecar and exact replay, original bank, raw Horlbeck source/library, and existing block assignments/predictions/results. No globally fitted GI or outcome correlations enter covariates. Processed gamma remains in original units. Use the original block splits and eligibility without exclusions; stop if exact target equivalence, counts, guide identities or split identity fail.

For each retained ordered construct and infection, read only exact arm-matched control-derived singles x in arm A and y in arm B, plus the same guides' opposite-arm control singles. Double-target values are used only for the frozen finite-field eligibility mask and target equivalence, never as covariates. Average features over identical ordered constructs, then equally over the two infections. Core features are sum, absolute difference, product, minimum and sum of squared singles. Extra features are physical-arm difference, squared difference, squared-single difference, sum of absolute arm shifts, sum and product of arm shifts, summed across-construct endpoint-single variance, squared minimum, construct count and absolute orientation imbalance. Arm shift is matched-arm single minus the same guide's opposite-arm single. Endpoint variance is mean squared single minus squared endpoint mean, summed over the canonical endpoints. Physical-arm signs preserve vector identity, while canonical query order cannot alter the feature aggregation.

The sidecar's active control partner bitsets and support counts are audited. If they are constant across all relevant guide/arm/infection cells, report their nonidentifiability as explanatory covariates and do not invent control effects. Variance and arm effects use control-only measurements. Guide/arm identity main effects cannot generally be separated from gene effects under a fixed library. Arbitrary canonical-pair technical effects are observationally indistinguishable from biological interactions without independent reagent changes or independent technical references, even if aligned completion wins.

## Fixed fitting and selection

Fit three ridge families with an unpenalized intercept: five core features, core plus additive gene incidence, and all fifteen features plus additive gene incidence. Center and scale using TRAIN only; train sufficient statistics in 2,048-row chunks. Penalties are exactly the existing 1, 10, 100, 1000 menu. Each family's minimum validation MSE selects its penalty; lowest validation MSE selects the baseline family, with ties in the listed family order. No capacity sweep, added features or test-based selection follows.

Fit the existing ScalarCompletion implementation to TRAIN residuals from the selected baseline. Rank eight, penalty .01, Adam learning rate .03, 500 steps, seed 20261022, train-only target standard deviation and validation checkpoint every 25 steps remain fixed. Fit three identical residual controls using seeds 20261023/24/25 and the existing TRAIN degree, single-effect and count strata. Permute TRAIN residuals only, keep graph fixed, and retain the baseline. Shared original-context summaries produce the same permutations across contexts. Require at least 80% permutable training pairs in every fold. Validation uses the genuine unshuffled residual for every checkpoint. Every baseline penalty/family/checkpoint choice for both contexts and all folds is saved before any new TEST scoring. No fit uses test labels. Simple fitting may see substantially more pair labels than any low-label claim; disclose counts per fold.

## Evaluation and unchanged gate

Use exactly the existing ordered union of 16,210 test pairs per context. Report MSE, MAE, bias, Pearson, target variance and both infection losses for every family, selected baseline, new aligned completion, three controls, frozen completion, frozen selected main effects, historical minimum, construct-first guide minimum and zero. Report paired aligned-completion squared loss minus selected nuisance baseline, frozen completion, nuisance-node family and every shuffled control. Use the existing 2,000-draw endpoint-multiplicity gene bootstrap, seed 20261022, with leave-one-gene-out influence and empty-draw counts. These are conditional gene-panel sensitivity intervals, not biological-replication confidence.

The primary gate requires at least 5% lower MSE than the validation-selected nuisance reference in BOTH contexts, negative upper conditional paired bound, no loss on either infection, lower MSE than all three informative shuffled controls, and no point-estimate loss against any fitted simple family or historical calibrated-main/minimum/zero reference. Every failure remains reported. No model promotion or molecular interaction claim follows even from passing. Frozen-completion comparison is descriptive and does not select a predictor.
