## SPIDR decision

The [official methods](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08815-4)
describe NT-based median-ratio normalization, T14/T0 LFC with pseudocount ten and
a modification that avoids a second normalization. These are normalized counts,
not raw integers or per-doubling gamma. The actual table-column/culture map and
ordered-construct key remain unverified; the published function definition does
not establish its invocation. The raw-count adapter must not ingest this table.
Do not round values, normalize them again, import outcome-selected guide filters,
or reuse the raw-unit T0 threshold without a normalized-unit registration.

Exact sequence, transcript, mismatch annotation, vector arm and explicit NT role
are retained in metadata joins. All 1,150 target guides have 15 designed NT
partners per arm; 225 NT/NT controls exist. The perfect/perfect primary has
121,198 two-construct designed pairs, not verified observed coverage. Target/NT
availability, T0 survival, missingness, count-key linkage and observed training
support remain unknown. Construct-first predictions, then equal surviving
construct/culture weighting and equal-pair scoring remain the required contract.
Metadata membership cannot establish any of these measured quantities.

Article/supplement CC BY 4.0 supports an attributed supplement-only route absent
an item-specific exception. A final item-notice check remains; unrelated FASTQ
redistribution rights are not a prerequisite for that route. The two cultures
share a transduction, so they cannot supply independently initiated replication.
Source-level laboratory separation does not remove within-screen confounding.

The missing artifact is a response-free companion that binds actual Table 8
sample columns to early/late cultures and ordered library keys, plus documented
normalization invocation/reference and control centering. After that, a normalized
endpoint, equal-information evaluator, full-design resource rehearsal, source-local
label budget and prospective test procedure still need qualification. The
synthetic evaluator audit neither supplies this schema nor certifies population
confidence. No reserved response access is authorized by a passing software test.

## A suitable alternative design, with a separate endpoint

[Shen et al. 2017](https://www.nature.com/articles/nmeth.4225) is a more suitable
graph design for supported-gene completion than a panel of isolated paralog
pairs. It assays every pair among 73 genes in HeLa, A549 and 293T, with nine
double-guide constructs per pair and 657 gene/scramble constructs. The
[primary methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5449203/) describe two
independent replicate experiments and four post-transduction times. This offers
local training edges for query genes and separately measured singles, with
published raw/processed assay data in Supplementary Table 2. No Table 2 bytes or
headers were requested. The article narrative, including its reported aggregate
outcomes, is now exposed; this is response-table reservation only.

This would be an adapted CRISPR-knockout growth-slope test. Derive singles only
from exact gene/scramble constructs, not the authors' robust all-partner probe
fitness fit, which can contain held-double information. It still needs verified
guide/arm, sample/time and replicate-initiation maps, licensing for the precise
supplement, train-safe count preprocessing and a new endpoint registration.
No reusable supplement-wide license was established in this review. Public
availability alone is insufficient to claim redistribution rights. The dense
design makes it a scientifically suitable qualification candidate, not an
already qualified confirmation source or gamma-transfer test.

The inspected [Dede enCas12a paralog design](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7558751/)
has measured single/control constructs but focuses on separate paralog pairs,
so nominal hundreds of pairs do not guarantee local graph support for held pairs.
Its control guides also target nonessential genes, which cannot silently become
NT controls. Do not substitute it to obtain an easier completion result.
SCHEMATIC retains its previously documented schema/rights and partner-derived
single issues. NCI-ALMANAC remains a distinct chemical/dose task, not genetic
confirmation. No alternative outcomes were acquired or scored.
