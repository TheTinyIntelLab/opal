# What the native NAIAD comparison can establish

Audit date: 21 October 2026. This review read the pinned official implementation, Opal's adapter, qualification receipts and registered block protocols without inspecting native biological scores or running models. It adds an interpretation record; `plan.md` remains the forward plan. No SPIDR response acquisition occurred.

The comparison can rank two fixed prediction procedures for locally supervised missing gene-pair blocks in six exposed scalar screens. It cannot establish source transfer, cold-gene prediction, prospective discovery efficiency or a new biological mechanism. A native win is useful evidence against assuming that the fixed rank-eight procedure is the strongest local predictor. A native loss would concern this registered recipe and task, not every NAIAD configuration.

## Why NAIAD belongs in this experiment

NAIAD conditions a scalar combination phenotype on measured single-perturbation effects and learned gene identities. Its published contribution includes adapting embedding capacity to data volume and recommending experiments, so conditioning on singles plus learned combination structure is established prior art. The final publication also includes a drug-combination extension. These are closer precedents for Opal's growth task than an expression decoder scored on a different endpoint. [ICML 2025 publication](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/qin25g.html), [official Apache-2.0 implementation](https://github.com/NeptuneBio/NAIAD).

The authors' accessible version-two preprint emphasizes small training budgets and sequential selection of strong-effect pairs. Its benchmark uses random pair partitions and downsampling, and its active-learning simulations optimize discovery rather than uniform error alone. Our dense local training and complete within-fold missing blocks answer a different question. We do not reproduce that benchmark or its recommendation claims. The preprint also states that measured individual effects are required and unseen genes are unsupported in the current model. [Authors' preprint, sections 4.2, 5 and 6](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.12010v2).

## What is matched, and what differs

The executable source is [the pinned official revision](https://github.com/NeptuneBio/NAIAD/tree/9207e9e855e865160a196a6989392f6db622b0d6), `9207e9e855e865160a196a6989392f6db622b0d6`. The adapter imports its architecture, dataset, optimizer, scheduler and trainer, rather than approximating them. Local inspection of [the adapter](../../src/naiad_reference.py), [scalar implementation](../../src/block_completion.py) and [native runner](../../tools/study_native_blocks.py) supports the following distinctions.

| Axis | Registered comparison and consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Observed information | Both receive the same eligible canonical pairs, local measured singles, training pair means and validation pair means. Both need incident training pairs for every query gene. Neither receives pathways, sequences, dose descriptors, context embeddings or target test outcomes during fitting. This matches information access for the local task. |
| Test problem | In each fit, all pairs joining two genes in one hash fold are withheld. Those genes still have training edges to other genes. The five test blocks are disjoint, but their fits share training observations, and cross-fold edges are outside the aggregate test set. This tests missing blocks among supported genes, not unseen genes or arbitrary whole-screen completion. |
| Fitting across screens | Every task receives its own parameters. Repeating this procedure in a later source is local adaptation. It cannot overturn the earlier failure of unchanged Horlbeck weights on external cohorts. |
| Native recipe | NAIAD uses one seed, 100 epochs, batch size 1,024, native capacity defaults, dropout, summed MSE, Adam and its warmup scheduler. No native hyperparameter search or ensemble runs. The selected checkpoint is the native strict validation minimum. These choices provide a reproducible default comparator, not evidence of native optimality. |
| Completion recipe | Completion fits a training-only selected simple reference and then a separately regularized rank-eight residual operator. It uses at most 500 full-batch steps and checks validation every 25 steps. The different objective, optimizer schedule and checkpoint frequency mean the experiment compares complete procedures rather than isolating architectural capacity. |
| Compute | Both use one CPU numerical thread, but native execution has a prospectively amended two-hour active budget and completion has one hour including its residual controls. Equal information is not equal compute or equal numbers of gradient updates. Report measured runtime separately from predictive accuracy. |
| Input and output adaptations | The wrapper bypasses native random resplitting, supplies only training and validation frames, and retains a seed-fixed training-row permutation. It averages both orders of the singles at inference because the native phenotype head is order-sensitive. Native checkpoint selection still uses its original shuffled single order. The reported object is native NAIAD architecture/trainer with symmetric inference, not an exact published-score reproduction. |
| Aggregation | Both predict the mean-replicate scalar with equal canonical-pair weights. Infection-specific losses are secondary checks on the same fitted prediction; they are not independent refits or new sources. Numerical state replay establishes artifact fidelity, not biological validity. |

The frozen [qualification protocol](naiad_protocol.md) contains a transcription error in its embedding-map sentence: nine dimensions are listed against ten thresholds. The pinned code actually maps `0:2, 2:2, 4:4, 10:16, 20:16, 30:32, 40:64, 60:64, 80:128, 100:128`, selecting the largest strictly lower threshold. The wrapper delegates this calculation to that code. Registered workload counts select 128 dimensions, so this correction changes no fitted recipe. Preserve the frozen protocol and use saved `model_args` as the execution receipt.

## Baselines and interpretation limits

The twelve registered references include zero, training mean, additive singles, minimum singles, four symmetric single calibrations and four gene-main-effect ridge penalties. The latter can exploit gene identity and main-effect regularities. Their validation-selected policy is stronger than an additive-only comparison, while the complete fixed menu exposes sensitivity without choosing a deployed baseline on test outcomes.

Ahlmann-Eltze and colleagues found simple linear and additive predictors competitive with deep transcriptomic models. Systema shows that shared perturbed-versus-control shifts can dominate expression metrics. Both support strong controls, but neither establishes that scalar growth interactions are absent. [Critical benchmark](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-025-02772-6.pdf), [Systema](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41587-025-02777-8).

Miller and colleagues subsequently show that positive controls and response-sensitive metrics can reveal transcriptomic advantages obscured by ordinary summary scores. Their DEG weighting addresses expression dimensions and cannot be copied directly into a single scalar outcome. It motivates qualifying the dynamic range of a future endpoint before evaluation; it does not authorize replacing this experiment's primary MSE after seeing results. [Published calibration study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-026-03307-w).

NAIAD beating ridge would not by itself isolate pair-specific interaction learning: its nonlinear singles head also changes. It would require a newly registered native phenotype-only comparison and appropriate identity or residual controls to attribute its gain. Q2's shuffled residual controls test the completion operator's assignment dependence, not NAIAD's mechanism. Conversely, beating shuffled labels only shows that the aligned assignments contain useful information beyond the preserved coarse strata; technical pair effects remain possible.

The endpoint-multiplicity gene bootstrap and leave-one-gene-out analysis describe conditional sensitivity to the observed gene panel. Five folds, six correlated tasks, guides and shared controls do not supply independent cultures. A point-estimate win or narrow conditional interval cannot become a population-level biological claim. Failure of the conservative all-six-task gate also does not prove that every narrower assay task is unlearnable. Any narrower successor selected after these outcomes needs new development registration and independent confirmation.

## What would add scientific value

Implementing a low-rank operator or the native NAIAD model is baseline engineering. A useful contribution would identify a capability that those procedures do not already establish, then demonstrate it with controlled information access and independent data. Plausible distinctions are reliable transfer across assay/reagent changes after a declared local calibration budget, calibrated abstention under such shift, or improved prospective discovery of reproducible interactions at a fixed experimental cost.

That evidence needs strong native and simple comparators, a practically meaningful registered gain, independent source or culture measurements, and guide-aware endpoints that separate reagent activity from gene identity. Discovery efficiency needs an explicit decision target and prospective confirmation; global MSE alone does not measure it. A frontier-level claim would require those gains to repeat across materially different settings with externally replayable artifacts and a capability beyond supported-gene interpolation. Current exposed block comparisons can choose the next experiment, but cannot supply that confirmation.
