## Limits and next decision

The comparison ranks complete procedures with matched observed information, not architectures at matched optimization or compute. Native uses its official architecture, defaults, optimizer and trainer with symmetric inference; its checkpoint is still selected by the native original-order validation loss. Completion uses a selected ridge reference and a separately regularized residual. Neither result reproduces the authors' published random-split/low-label benchmark, and a native loss here does not establish an optimized-method loss. See [the comparability record](native_comparability.md).

Both methods have access to many local pair labels. Their common advantage over the registered simple menu may contain a nonlinear function of measured singles, guide/control weighting, vector-arm effects or technical pair effects. Coarse nuisance-stratified residual shuffles do not preserve every nonlinear singles function. The next discriminating work is exact-guide/arm-aware baseline QA and one separately registered flexible single-only comparison, before adding biological priors or opening reserved response data. Local missing-block gains do not repair the historical failure of unchanged weights under source transfer.

All six tasks remain exposed development sources; later cohorts overlap and share laboratory lineage. Five folds reuse training labels, two infections share fitted predictors, and guides/controls are dependent. None is a new independent biological replicate. Family choice reuses the validation data already used for checkpoint/reference selection and was proposed after Q2 scores, so it cannot be promoted as an independent confirmation or a new discovery method. A practical capability still needs a declared local-label cost, an untouched qualified source and appropriate biological validation.
