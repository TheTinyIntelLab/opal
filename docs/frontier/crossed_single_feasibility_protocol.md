# Registered crossed-single feasibility study

`opal_crossed_single_v1` is a separate exposed-development spending probe under the PLAN's single-response pivot. It predicts withheld compound effects in one of three observed cell lines using measured effects of the same compounds in the other two. Target training identities provide calibration. It does not test unseen intervention identity, unseen-context adaptation, pair composition or independent biological confirmation. The owner has authorized execution and selected this practical route after pair-source qualification failed. The frozen pair contract remains unchanged.

The numeric registration is `configs/experiments/opal_crossed_single_v1.json`. It was written before new scores. The local corrected sci-Plex raw counts and primary pData must match their pinned SHA256 identities; the existing zero-mismatch provenance audit must match every cell. The [primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7289078/) documents the dose/time design and randomized treatment wells. The [corrected release](https://zenodo.org/records/13350497) is the declared CC-BY-4.0 source. Technical plate/well repeats do not establish independently initiated culture confirmation.

Use the first 96 lexicographically shared compound names at exactly 1,000 nM and 24 hours, three fixed lines A549/K562/MCF7, and 512 control-only genes from the existing preparation rule. Query vehicles are permitted for panel selection and subtraction. No treated query response chooses genes. Normalize each cell by its human-gene UMI total to 10,000, apply log2(1+x), average wells and subtract the exact line/plate vehicle, then average available plates equally within line. This preserves the existing exposed single-response endpoint and is explicitly different from the pair contract's mean-before-log scale. The full raw cache is neither copied nor downloaded.

For each target line, concatenate the other two measured source profiles in fixed line order. A centered training-identity-only PCA has exactly 16 coordinates; reject effective rank below 16 or condition above 1e8 at relative tolerance 1e-10. Normalize coordinate RMS on training identities only. Fit a full-output ridge to target response minus the mean of the two source effects, with a training-only gene intercept. The objective averages squared residual loss over training identities and uses penalties 0.1/1/10. Three globally grouped compound folds and three refitted inner folds use seed 20261014. The same compound fold allocation is shared across all target directions, so dose aliases cannot cross folds.

References are zero, measured source mean, target training mean, shared offset, scalar affine calibration, equal-rank source-mean and source-difference PCA ridges, and a source-profile RBF residual kernel with training-distance median width. They use identical training/calibration labels and the same penalty menu where applicable. One source-feature alignment control, seed 20261015, has the same rank/penalty/offset freedom. All three target contexts contain permitted training outcomes, so these scores are observed-context missing-response predictions. The locked reference selector excludes the concatenated candidate and its shuffled control; ties use family ID then penalty. There are 22 specifications, ten selected families, 684 logical invocations, 612 learned fits and 360 PCA constructions across the full run.

Every nested and final state, unsupported attempt, kernel bank, basis, scaler and prediction is retained with safe content-addressed JSON/NPZ. Fold fitting/query/evaluator files are physically separate. All predictions and exact saved-state replays are sealed before evaluator files are loaded. Unsupported families use the locked-reference prediction on the complete declared query population and block the spending filter. There is no deletion of difficult compounds or rescue rank/seed/dose.

Primary MSE averages genes then held compound identities, separately for each target line. A single set of 2,000 whole-compound draws couples all target contexts, seed 20261016; paired loss bounds are conditional sensitivity on exposed measurements and selection. They are not biological or procedure-refitting intervals. The finite probe passes its development spending filter only if the candidate improves selector MSE by at least 5% in every line, has a positive conditional paired lower bound, beats all declared mean/difference/kernel/alignment controls, and has no required unsupported fit. This new spending filter does not pass the pair protocol's five gates or nominate an independently confirmed procedure.

The complete single-process budget is two CPU hours, 8 GB RAM, 1 GB retained outputs, 2 GB scratch allowance, 10 MB failure reserve and at least 1 GiB writable reserve. The existing preparer has an additional 30-minute wall stop. One numerical thread/worker, no installs/downloads/GPU. This is a finite layout under persistent owner authorization; CPU, RSS and retained bytes are measured for the actual process. Neither the 16-coordinate head nor compressed predictions alone represent inference cost.
