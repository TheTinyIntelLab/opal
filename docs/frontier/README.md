- [Evidence audit](evidence_audit_20261015.md) separates numerical replay from biological independence and source rights.
- [Workstream closeout](workstream_closeout.md) records completed measurement recovery and the retained failures.

- [Context-contrast implementation](context_contrast_implementation.md) preserves the unqualified source contract and software scope.
- [Crossed-single results](crossed_single_feasibility_results.md) report the failed spending criterion under their declared information budget.
- [Current v1 findings](../research.md) distinguish later assay-local growth results from unsuccessful external transfer.

Superseded plans, coordination messages and status snapshots have been removed from these folders. Byte-preserved scientific history is available through the [archive index](../archive/README.md). Private planning and contact logs remain outside the public repository.
