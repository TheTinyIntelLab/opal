## Decision

Opal's most defensible frontier opportunity is **reliable prediction of reproducible, context-dependent combination effects**, eventually used to choose combinations that change a prespecified biological program. Its first decisive experiment should test whether crossed, repeated measurements contain transferable pair-specific information beyond calibrated singles and intervention main effects. Building a general context-transfer transformer immediately is poorly justified: PRESAGE, TxPert, State, Stack and the newly released PIE already occupy that territory, and Opal's existing state heads and projected variance heads failed their own gates.

This is an inference about the opportunity, not a claim that no published model can predict combinations. GEARS, CPA, NAIAD, PertSpectra and CellFlow address combinations in different ways. The unmet claim is narrower: independently replicated, pair-specific transcriptomic superiority under a **globally unseen pair and held biological context**, with honest uncertainty and demonstrated experimental utility. The literature inspected does not convincingly establish that combined claim. Absence of such evidence in this investigation is not proof of universal absence.

## What Opal evidence changes

The manuscript's historical neural gains over additivity do not establish a pair-specific contribution. Norman's selected network-versus-simple comparison is inconclusive; Wessels favors a global correction. Later Interaction gains are below 0.1%, and Guard is not promoted. D-SPIN transfers a selected *procedure*, refitted on source contexts, rather than EmeraldBay weights; it fails superiority and its nominal 90% source radius covers 34.3% under shift. Those distinctions prohibit promising that a larger version of the same architecture will solve transfer.

The variance report corroborates the last-page manuscript results: TNF-positive partner variance improves Base by 0.231163%, but equally tuned mean and capacity controls do better; TNF-negative partner models fall back for insufficient nested support. This rejects that projection/head/mean-endpoint experiment. It does not reject covariance, state mixtures, perturbation penetrance, temporal heterogeneity or a cell-distribution endpoint. None of those alternatives should inherit a positive claim from the failed experiment.

Local comparison status is also restrictive. GEARS and CPA aggregate inputs, signed-profile SLIM, and graph-free PertSpectra are adaptations. PRESAGE's unresolved decoder/pooling/pair-output issues, TxPert's unresolved environment closure and PertAdapt's memory/NCCL failures establish execution limits rather than inferiority. The report states zero fully eligible contemporary native comparisons. Frontier claims need new native-contract evaluations, not a table recycled from these adaptations.

## Relevant alternatives discovered

[CellOT](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-023-01969-x) learns transport between control and perturbed distributions and makes heterogeneity explicit. It is a native distribution comparator if that becomes the claim; unpaired snapshot transport does not identify real individual-cell trajectories. [CIPHER](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12363937/) is especially relevant to variance/covariance ideas because it connects fluctuation structure to perturbation response; Opal's projected diagonal variance experiment is not a test of its complete assumptions or algorithm.

The search also surfaced recent [MultiFlow](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.08.20.746112v1.full), [scEGFlow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02232), and [CisTransCell](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13713). Their primary abstracts concern multimodal flow, energy-guided distribution prediction, and regulatory/context priors respectively; they were not fully audited here and should enter the day-7 comparator qualification queue rather than inherit an asserted superiority rank. [CLAMP author code](https://github.com/AmayaGS/CLAMP-Perturbseq) shows that fitting mechanistic steady-state regulatory operators through frozen encoders is also occupied prior art. A graph/operator proposal needs comparison and a falsifiable assumption, not a mechanism label alone.

## Three unresolved capabilities, and the opportunity ranking

1. **Replicated joint composition/context prediction.** Predict a pair never used as a pair anywhere in fitting, in a separately held biological context, recover its deviation from an explicitly calibrated singles-only reference, and confirm it in independently initiated cultures. Existing work addresses components of this problem, but the combined native claim remains underdemonstrated in the audited sources. This is closest to Opal's expertise and most distinct from generic State/PIE transfer. Its bottleneck is crossed, replicated data.
2. **Useful distributional combination prediction.** Recover response mixture weights, penetrance and tail events for unseen combinations under context shift, rather than merely sample cells with a correct mean. CPA/CellFlow/CellOT and new flows strongly constrain novelty. A narrow benefit tied to a measurable scientific program could still be frontier, but Opal has not yet qualified an endpoint or training regime for it.
3. **Reliable prospective decision making under shift.** Rank or select combinations with controlled false discoveries and materially fewer assays, confirmed on a fresh culture block. NAIAD already provides a strong active-learning precedent; Opal must show biological replication, context-dependent transcriptomic objectives and realized cost reduction. Failed shifted intervals mean uncertainty-driven selection cannot be an immediate validated product.

Rank one first, with rank three as the eventual utility test. Rank two should be a contingency if independently repeated means show weak predictable residual signal but a prespecified distributional endpoint has reproducible effects. Do not change endpoints merely because mean prediction loses.

## Minimum experiment before a successor architecture

Freeze a crossed intervention-by-context panel with matched controls/singles and simultaneous pairs at one exact dose/time/modal contract. Qualify intervention/reagent identity, initiation batches and source rights before outcome access. First determine whether pair-specific departures from a calibrated singles/main-effects reference repeat in held cultures and transfer between contexts. Use a compact source response representation and closed-form symmetric residual head, with a source-average pair residual and an information-matched kernel as challengers. Permute external identity representations and source-response matching while preserving their dimensions, amount of supervision and target frequencies.

The information question is whether **correct source-context intervention response matching adds held-context pair information** beyond permitted query singles and simple calibration. A larger contextual model is justified only if the correct matching beats shuffled matching, capacity controls and the best matched simple model, with gains surviving a globally held pair/context population and replicate-aware intervals. A gain explained by reference shrinkage or error cancellation does not justify a new pair-interaction claim.

Before calling this novel, run PIE/PRESAGE or their faithfully disclosed pair adaptations and native GEARS/CPA/PertSpectra where eligible. Competitors that cannot accept the task should be labeled incompatible, with native transfer capabilities discussed separately. Restrict a future frontier claim to the exact combination domain rather than claiming a universal virtual cell.

## Evaluation literature changes the decision

[Ahlmann-Eltze, Huber and Anders, Nature Methods 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-025-02772-6) shows why additive and linear references and interaction-specific analysis matter. [Miller et al., Nature Biotechnology, 1 October 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-026-03307-w) shows that common metrics can fail to discriminate informative prediction; this prevents equating every negative bulk MSE comparison with biological impossibility. Together they imply a prespecified metric qualification stage with exact/oracle, independent-repeat, zero, constant and identity-shuffled controls, while retaining strong quantitative references. Metric calibration cannot be used to erase an unfavorable frozen endpoint, and positive controls belong in evaluator custody.

## Actual cross-examination and revised recommendation

Reviewed `B_data.md`, `C_architecture.md` and the root `experiment_contract.md` on 11 October 2026 after the independent investigation.

**Review of B.** I agree that crossed, independently initiated pair/context data are the most valuable new information and that reported repeats cannot automatically become cultures. The inventory correctly distinguishes Tahoe singles from a combinatorial screen and protects against generated-data confirmation. I disagree with making a new Tahoe single-response model the dominant first experiment. It could qualify a context representation cheaply, but source single-transfer success is neither necessary nor sufficient for transferable pair residuals, while PIE, PRESAGE, TxPert and State directly compete on its task. It would therefore introduce a separate unknown before testing the scientific capability Opal needs. Keep Tahoe as the predefined *alternative representation branch* if the few-shot composition experiment is infeasible or identifies a specific source-representation bottleneck; compare to PIE/native public baselines before attaching novelty to it. B's recommendation to estimate culture precision and empirical power before a large pair panel materially improves my original proposal: I now explicitly defer a full laboratory panel until a small independently initiated measurement pilot qualifies repeatable signal and a useful effect margin.

**Review of C.** Its separation of measurement reproducibility, added information and model capacity is correct, and its four candidates have distinct failure conditions. The preferred context-contrast model has a hidden difficulty: with only two contexts, a change-to-change mapping cannot establish held-context generality; a predicted source-average residual must never use the query pair in another context, or the apparent capability is pair lookup transfer. The draft contract repairs both by globally excluding query pairs and declaring two-direction TNF results one exposed feasibility contrast. I prefer its response-MSE primary endpoint over C's initial contrast-MSE primary: a model that predicts centered contrasts while worsening complete response may have no useful capability. C's contrast-MSE and absolute-error safeguards should remain attribution diagnostics rather than another independent success claim. Its proposed 10% contrast gate is not a biologically justified frontier standard, and neither is the root 5% response gate; both are administrative spending decisions until empirical independent-culture power and utility define a meaningful margin.

**Root contract assessment.** The rank-two source basis, four identity-selected bridges, shared gene-vector intercept and mandatory source-free/mean/affine/incidence/kernel controls make the first experiment narrow and falsifiable. Four bridges are target pair measurements, so the claim is *few-shot composition transfer*, not zero-shot context transfer. The interception vector has hundreds of fitted values despite two adapted mode coefficients, so complete degrees of freedom/storage accounting and equal-intercept baseline access are necessary; the draft includes them. A rank-two supported adapter losing ends this finite representation family, while unsupported rank or reference/guide incompatibility produces an infeasible result. The 36-common-pair feasibility threshold is an engineering contract and cannot be presented as statistical power. A positive exposed TNF result warrants a qualified independent measurement pilot; it does not establish new biology or a frontier model.

**Novelty revision: BATCHIE is a stronger active-learning precedent than my initial report included.** The [January 2025 Nature Communications paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-55287-7) reports prospective combination screening of 206 drugs in pediatric cancer lines, using Bayesian tensor factorization and batch information gain. Thus “prospective combination discovery with uncertainty” is already demonstrated prior art in scalar viability. [Author code](https://github.com/tansey-lab/batchie) is [MIT](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tansey-lab/batchie/main/LICENSE), and the [archived screen data](https://zenodo.org/records/12764821) are available. Opal's proposed distinction must be verified transcriptomic/program composition across held contexts with replication and global pair exclusion, not active acquisition itself. A transcriptomic BATCHIE head is an adaptation; if the eventual objective becomes viability, native BATCHIE is a mandatory competitor.

**Native competitor duty.** The first CPU feasibility experiment need not import every large method; strong matched simple controls can reject it cheaply. Before promotion to a specialized superiority claim, native-compatible GEARS/CPA/PertSpectra and a faithful public-prior SLIM/PRESAGE/PIE comparator must be attempted under audited information, label budgets and adequate training recipes. Models that lack simultaneous-pair handling can provide a clearly labeled additive/single-response adaptation or a separate native task, but cannot silently disappear from the strategic comparison. BATCHIE/NAIAD enter the later acquisition/phenotype stage. Environment failure, proprietary-resource absence and licensing limits must remain explicit comparison gaps. Large-model or acquisition investment remains blocked on positive, independently replicated information evidence.

The revised primary course is therefore the root's bounded **sparse-bridge composition-transfer experiment**, followed by a separately qualified independent-culture pilot if it passes. This narrows my initial context-transfer proposal and adopts B's measurement/power qualification. Meaningful disagreement remains: B prefers Tahoe single-transfer first; I consider that a secondary task with weaker differentiation and weaker direct relevance to the combination hypothesis.
