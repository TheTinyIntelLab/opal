# Specialist D: evaluation and claim contract

Date: 2026-10-11. Status: independent initial recommendation; proposed protocol, not executed science.

## Verdict

The next useful test is whether sparse measured target-context bridge pairs convey transferable, pair-specific interaction information beyond recalibrating measured singles. Globally holding query pair identities out of every source context is essential: transferring the same pair from another context is a different and substantially easier capability. A positive result would justify an adaptation model; it would not yet justify a frontier claim.

## Verified local evidence and its limits

Inspected the extracted current manuscript at `tmp/frontier_manuscript.txt`, `src/evaluation/__init__.py`, `src/replicated/evaluation.py`, `src/replicated/contracts.py`, `src/replicated/protocol.py`, and `configs/experiments/opal_information_v1.json`. The 43-page manuscript contains explicit claim restrictions that must survive synthesis. Some large text outputs were truncated, so this review does not certify an independent line-by-line check of every appendix or numerical artifact.

The existing general evaluator computes equal-pair MSE, MAE, additive skill, residual cosine, response Pearson, and residual norm error. Its pair/target-product intervals explicitly omit biological replication and shared-control uncertainty. Its technical bootstrap correctly reuses one sampled control and one sampled single per stratum/target across affected pairs. Those are useful diagnostics, not biological confidence intervals.

The replicated evaluator collapses technical observations before equal-culture/equal-pair aggregation and uses a paired Student interval on independent culture mean errors. This supports a fixed panel in a fixed state; it does not identify performance on new target/context populations. `src/replicated/protocol.py` explicitly rejects mixing states, assay/time, and component dose/reagent assignments for its scientific role. A bridge/context study therefore requires a separate contract and inference implementation rather than assigning a broad claim to the existing fixed-state routine. Three cultures are its software minimum, not a demonstrated sufficient sample size. Power records themselves retain `confirmation_design_qualified=false` until empirical independent-culture evidence qualifies a design.

The variance protocol compares 12 configurations per arm plus zero correction, capacity-matched mean features, and within-compatible-stratum shuffled variances under nested partner folds. It asks whether projected single-cell variance helps **mean** prediction. The appendix reports 0.231163% TNF-positive partner improvement over Base, less than mean controls; TNF-negative uses fallback for insufficient support. This fails the registered 2% administrative screen. It neither establishes that biological heterogeneity is irrelevant nor that distribution prediction failed. Same-anchor residual MSE is algebraically identical to response MSE, so relabeling it an interaction score cannot establish interaction recovery.

## Current evaluation literature

[Ahlmann-Eltze, Huber and Anders, Nature Methods 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-025-02772-6) establishes that strong linear references are mandatory under its evaluated protocols, not that complex models are universally useless.

[Miller et al., Nature Biotechnology 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-026-03307-w) finds rankings depend on metric calibration and distinguishes control bias from diluted perturbation signal. Positive and negative controls measure metric sensitivity; some models beat uninformative references on calibrated metrics. The paper recommends complementary metric families, retains direct error as a realism guardrail, and does not evaluate unseen-context prediction. Its DEG-based diagnostics and duplicate predictions use held outcomes under evaluator-only access, so they must not enter model selection or claimed inference inputs.

[Nicol, Shivakumar and Irizarry, bioRxiv 2026](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.07.723486v1) shows shared control subtraction can inflate differential-expression correlation even for noninformative predictions. Separate control samples for prediction and evaluator delta calculations are consequently a required diagnostic; a correlation gain alone cannot earn promotion.

## Benchmark capability matrix

| Task | Permitted query information | Required exclusion | Meaning of success |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seen singles, unseen pair | Exact matched query singles and controls | All query pair labels within evaluated context | Conditional composition interpolation |
| Partner holdout | Held target singles remain measured | Every training pair incident to held targets | Generalization beyond learned partner incidence; not unseen intervention |
| Global pair holdout | Query singles and nonquery bridge pairs | Query canonical pair in all contexts, donors, batches and doses for identity-level claim | New combination identity |
| Unseen intervention | Descriptor/prior only for withheld identity | Withheld identity outcomes, singles and all incident pairs everywhere | New identity prediction; must estimate main effects separately |
| Held context | Query controls/singles or controls only, declared separately | Query-context outcomes except declared bridge budget | Few-shot adaptation or zero-shot transfer according to inputs |
| Joint pair/context shift | Query references and fixed bridge budget | Held query pair everywhere; target contexts not used for hyperparameter selection | Novel pair in new state after limited adaptation |
| Dose interpolation | Exact allowed lower/upper dose responses | Interior held dose outcomes | Known intervention/dose interpolation |
| Dose extrapolation | Allowed lower-range doses only | High-dose outcomes throughout fitting/selection | Out-of-range dose prediction; no substitute for identity transfer |
| Higher order | Measured singles and explicitly allowed lower-order subsets | Held triplet and any label giving its full outcome | Higher-order composition; only when matched physical treatments exist |

Historical protected Norman/Wessels tests remain untouched. Exposed TNF, EmeraldBay, D-SPIN and other inspected records can support developmental falsification, not fresh confirmation. No task without adequate real matched support enters the headline benchmark.

## Small decisive bridge study

Freeze `frontier_bridge_v1`, exact dose/time/reagent identities, ordered output genes, contexts, controls, shared singles, bridge identities, and global query-pair blacklist. Fit a source residual predictor and source-only output basis; permit a low-dimensional target adaptation fit to bridge residuals only. Do not also introduce pretrained representations, distributional objectives, or active acquisition in this first test.

Use metadata-only target-balanced bridge selection, fixed budgets 0, 4, 8 and 16 eligible pair identities; choose one feasible nonzero primary budget before outcomes. Budget counts distinct conditions and full assay cost, not pair-stratum rows. Feasibility requires enough disjoint globally held query identities and connected bridge incidence support. Report bridge feature matrix rank, condition number, target degrees, component coverage and unseen components. Sparse adaptation with more coefficients than effective observations is a failure of identifiability; reduce rank or decline the claim.

Every arm gets the same source labels, query singles, controls and bridge outcomes: (1) additive; (2) source-only frozen Base/kernel; (3) bridge shared offset; (4) bridge scalar/diagonal affine calibration of additive response; (5) ridge component-incidence/main-effect calibration; (6) target-only ridge/kernel with bridge labels; (7) pooled source-plus-bridge kernel; (8) source predictor plus bridge adaptation. Match the adaptation coefficient budget and tuning effort wherever meaningful. Use a source-to-target repeated-pair predictor as an explicitly forbidden-information positive control, never a deployment baseline.

Controls: permute bridge residual assignments to bridge identities within matched context/stratum while preserving the same outcome multiset, budget, amplitude, training count and tuning; shuffle source context labels; randomize source pair identity alignment; fit adaptation after subtracting source-trained global/affine/incidence correction; zero bridge correction; simulated pure affine distortion with no interaction transfer; planted pair-specific low-rank transferable signal; independent-control scoring. Shuffle incompatibility or singleton strata must be declared, not repaired by pooling biologically different observations. Synthetic controls verify identifiability and code only.

Primary endpoint: equal-pair gene-averaged response MSE within context/culture, then equal eligible context weighting, relative to a development-selected **same-bridge-information** strongest simple reference. The principal mechanistic attribution is a prespecified independently anchored interaction-departure metric: evaluator pair effect minus evaluator independently measured singles, after development-fixed calibration terms; prediction uses separate permitted references. This differs from subtracting the same noisy additive vector from prediction and truth. Report decomposition into main-effect error, residual error and cross term so cancellation remains visible.

Mandatory secondary discriminative endpoint: normalized inverse retrieval rank over the full preregistered query population after subtracting a training/bridge-only context mean correction, with candidate pool fixed across methods; report query-response correlation across perturbations for each gene, undefined cases and constant predictions. Calibrate these diagnostics with held-out identity-shuffled/constant predictions and disjoint technical duplicates. Do not trim test conditions because a metric fails calibration; mark that metric unqualified for the claim. Evaluator-only DEG/emergent panels may be added through independent halves or independent cultures, but do not alter the fixed primary output universe.

Distributional prediction is a separate claim: use held cell distributions, energy distance/MMD in a frozen independently justified feature space and cell-state proportion errors; compare conditional mean resampling, empirical controls and covariance-matched Gaussian references. Matching a centroid or winning retrieval does not show distribution recovery. Experimental utility is another claim: use prespecified independently measured discovery hit definitions and assay-budget discovery curves, not just MSE reduction.

## Statistical and promotion contract

Development: nested context holds and global pair partitions, source-only selection, adaptation using bridge training outcomes, and sealed query outcomes. Context holdout must group all culture batches of the biological context. Repeat split seeds as sensitivity, never count them or overlapping partner folds as independent replicates. Keep complete query populations with declared fallback/abstention; report coverage and full-denominator risk.

Confirmation: freeze procedure before fresh independently initiated cultures and untouched contexts are measured. For a fixed panel in one state, culture paired intervals are appropriate. For a claim over both contexts and targets, resample independent context groups, independent cultures within context, and target identities with incidence-aware product weights, reusing whole control/single blocks. With too few independent contexts/targets for validated coverage, report individual context effects and call population inference unavailable. Validate interval coverage and null false-positive rate in prespecified dependence-preserving simulations; repeat full fitting/selection when the estimand is the training procedure rather than fixed model. Do not claim conformal guarantees under arbitrary domain shift.

Proposed developmental advancement gate: >=5% response-MSE reduction over the locked same-information simple selector at the fixed primary bridge budget, positive improvement against identity-shuffled bridge adaptation, and positive independently anchored interaction-discrimination gain in both qualified context directions. This 5% is an administrative cost filter until a collaborator defines useful biological precision; it is not a frontier definition. Full-population MSE must not worsen materially in any tested context; register the acceptable noninferiority margin before scoring. If there is only one context contrast, permit prototype investigation only.

Falsification: with qualified measurement and adequate support, an upper uncertainty bound below the useful gain margin, or loss of gains after matched affine/incidence calibration, rejects the interaction-adaptation hypothesis. Wide uncertainty is inconclusive, triggering only the preregistered additional measurement plan, not unbounded architecture search. Gains on same source-pair transfer that disappear under global pair holdout reject the new-combination capability while possibly preserving a narrower repeated-pair adaptation tool.

## Frontier standard

A prototype reproducibly beats strong matched-input references for a precisely named task and survives controls; it may remain retrospective. A strong specialized model repeats superiority on meaningful held-context/global-pair shifts and independent cultures, using compatible current competitors with their actual resource/input tiers. A frontier specialized model adds independent external replication, externally replayable artifacts, prospective useful discovery or prediction in its claimed domain, and competitive current native comparators under equivalent tasks and information. Neither parameter count nor a single MSE percentage establishes that level. For this program the possible narrow claim is **sample-efficient transfer of reproducible combination-specific responses to new biological contexts and globally unmeasured pair identities**, conditional on measured singles and a declared bridge budget.

## Disagreement to retain

Sparse bridge adaptation is promising information acquisition, but the repository contains no demonstrated independent biological interaction signal that guarantees it will work. It may only learn state-dependent calibration. The first decisive study must expose that possibility; building a transformer before resolving it spends compute on a question of data and identifiability.

## Actual cross-examination: E and G, and revised contract

I read `E_utility.md`, `G_redteam.md`, and the integrated `experiment_contract.md` after writing the independent report. Both reviews materially improve the program. The integrated four-bridge, rank-two, fixed-penalty experiment supersedes my initial exploratory 0/4/8/16 budget menu: use exactly four as the primary and zero as a diagnostic; do not add budgets after inspecting results.

**E review.** E correctly separates retrieval discrimination from experimental usefulness. I adopt a frozen source-derived or collaborator-defined transcriptomic-program precision@k/simple-regret diagnostic, with a fixed full query pool and prespecified k; therapeutic or causal-function claims additionally need an independent functional assay. It remains secondary in the first cycle, because adding utility as another primary would entangle representation transfer with an unvalidated biological objective. E's future campaign proposal must freeze the hit threshold and competing policy lists before outcomes and define whether overlapping recommended pairs are counted once for total cost. Its proposed source-free equal-capacity adapter is necessary: otherwise a source-basis gain over an underpowered target kernel could be attributed to regularization rather than transferred source correspondence. Bridge component overlap counts 0/1/2 are frozen metadata diagnostics; absence of the zero-overlap subgroup narrows the claim and never prompts an outcome-dependent redraw.

**G review.** G correctly identifies shared gene-vector intercept freedom, rank deficiency, weak TNF-negative support, and the need to compare new bridge labels with improved independent reference precision. I accept relative singular tolerance `1e-8`, effective rank two, condition number <=`1e4`, source-only RMS scaling, gene-averaged loss, and reported leave-one-bridge sensitivity as implementation refusal/stability rules. These qualify numerics, not biological sample size. G's reference-precision alternative is a serious disagreement: the public pilot can report matched reference subsampling as sensitivity, but choosing four bridge outcomes versus four independent cultures is a biological cost allocation question that retrospective cell resampling cannot answer. Include both options in a collaborator's pilot cost/design review rather than introducing another target-selected public arm. G's statement that an uncertainty-usable acquisition policy warrants prospective comparison needs a separate utility test: calibrated uncertainty can still select unhelpful experiments, and a useful fixed ranking policy may work without uncertainty calibration.

**Precise permissible public sensitivity interval.** First produce one fixed prediction per globally held canonical pair, stratum and direction; source selection, bridge membership/fitting, estimated references and output modes remain fixed. Average gene squared error per row. Each of 2,000 draws samples the unique eligible target identities with replacement, giving count m(t); use pair weight m(a)m(b), reusing the same counts for every stratum, fold, method and both directions. Pool each canonical query pair once per eligible stratum across its exclusive global fold, compute weighted loss within stratum, then equal-stratum loss separately for each direction. Do not accidentally count the fold predictions multiple times or bootstrap repeated evaluation events. Record every zero-denominator draw; intervals are unavailable if support produces too few valid common draws under a prespecified rule. Use the paired reference-minus-candidate difference percentile interval and report ratio gain separately; do not scale a difference interval into a ratio confidence interval without labeling the fixed-denominator approximation. Report draws, valid draws, target degrees and effective weighted sample sizes. This is a conditional target-incidence sensitivity range, not a confidence interval for independent biological transfer, because references and fitted procedure are fixed and target-product weighting is not itself a validated population sampling model.

Use identical valid draws for both directions. The integrated gate requires each direction's point gain >=5% and each paired-difference lower sensitivity bound >0, plus fixed control/discrimination requirements. The two directions are dependent; never combine their p values or multiply their success probabilities. Requiring both conditions is an intersection gate, not two independent replications. This conservative administrative rule may reject a useful asymmetric transfer system, so retain the direction-specific results and permit a later separately hypothesized asymmetric task only with fresh evidence.

**Qualified biological null versus weak/infeasible public results.** Metadata failure, missing matched references, insufficient common pairs, rank failure, or unsupported independent-reference diagnostics produce explicit unqualified flags. They stop this finite public operator run but do not falsify biological transfer. A supported fit that misses its 5% spending gate is a negative result for this architecture, budget and exposed deposit; narrow/wide conditional intervals do not turn it into a powered biological null. Before expensive expansion, independent culture pilot data must show reproducible pair departures beyond shared/affine/incidence correction under leave-one-culture-out evaluation, exact treatment/reference matching, and scientifically useful response/program precision. Empirical covariance must support a fixed confirmatory sample size whose validated inference has adequate power for the collaborator-defined margin, acceptable null false-positive behavior and interval coverage under the stated dependence. If measurement cannot qualify, improve the experimental design or stop for lack of identifiable information. If a qualified, powered, frozen independent confirmation has an upper interval bound below that useful margin, abandon the tested few-shot interaction-transfer strategy. A failed public screen may justify a measurement pilot to distinguish signal absence from unreliable measurement, but does not authorize a larger model.

The substantive disagreement remains: E favors usefulness as the eventual decision objective, G doubts bridges outperform spending on reference precision, and I prioritize identifiable pair/context transfer before a utility claim. These are separate empirical questions; the finite bridge prototype resolves only whether source-conditioned structure adds predictive value to a fixed four-label adaptation contract.
