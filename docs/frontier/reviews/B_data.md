# B — Data and information investigation

Date: 11 October 2026. Independent specialist review. Local manuscript: `paper/main.pdf`, using the complete 43-page extract `tmp/frontier_manuscript.txt`. Repository paths below are relative to the Opal checkout. This review does not consume new expression matrices or reopen protected evaluators.

## Recommendation

The most valuable new information source is a **crossed intervention-pair × biological-context panel with exact matched singles and independently initiated cultures**. It would distinguish reproducible context-dependent composition from the shared corrections that dominate current Opal. More cells, graphs, projected variance or parameters cannot substitute for this design. A collaborator must supply it; no examined public source currently supplies broad pair coverage, multiple contexts and sufficient documented culture replication together.

The most informative immediate public-data experiment is smaller: learn a transferable single-response representation on a bounded, crossed subset of Tahoe-100M, and test whether it predicts the same known intervention in held cell lines better than intervention means, low-rank linear transfer and control-conditioned ridge/kernel references. This is a new task qualification experiment, not evidence of combination prediction. It is justified only if its central information difference is explicitly **many crossed contexts/interventions**, rather than another architecture on the already failed three-line sci-Plex or small EmeraldBay regime. Pair transfer follows only after this first test passes. I prefer this public precursor to immediately commissioning a large pair panel because it can cheaply falsify the proposed context representation before laboratory expenditure. I do not infer that single-transfer success would imply pair-transfer success.

## Which information matters, and which claim it could support

**Coverage** supplies independent intervention combinations, while more cells chiefly improve each conditional estimate. Add random non-star edges and held-partner support; a trametinib star provides valuable anchor-specific prediction but no broad two-intervention composition identification. Pair coverage should be increased only with exact reference and replicate contracts.

**Crossed context and intervention** is the missing information for disentangling intervention identity from biological susceptibility. Tahoe offers many lines repeatedly exposed to the same drugs, whereas the existing three-line sci-Plex test cannot establish how transfer scales with diverse contexts. A line-wide holdout must exclude every drug/dose/replicate of that line, including pretrained response exposure. Cell-line identity or co-culture effects are not patient-state generalization.

**Independent initiation** establishes repeatability and permits biological covariance estimates. It has greater scientific value than relabeling sequencing lanes or pooled cells as replicates. Two or three reported repeats can support transparent retrospective comparisons; the old six-culture requirement arises from one allocation/procedure and is not a universal law. Freeze a separate empirical-power design rather than lowering that old gate after seeing results.

**Distributions** require control/single/pair cell counts and a baseline-defined population. Projected single variance is neither sufficient representation nor an identified transport map. Independent endpoint snapshots cannot identify cellwise trajectories. A successful distribution model must beat mean-shifted empirical controls and resampled distribution references on calibrated distribution metrics before invoking a flow architecture.

**Chemistry, targets and graphs** can connect otherwise isolated intervention identities. Tahoe supplies SMILES but missing/antibody structures need explicit masks; publisher MoA was partly GPT-curated and must not be called independently verified biology. Frozen structure fingerprints or graph embeddings should first improve identity/target-family holds beyond scaffold-neighbor/class-mean references. Randomized graphs and dose/name-shuffled controls distinguish external knowledge from flexible correction. Gene graphs do not supply missing experimental pair effects.

**Dose/time** adds a different extrapolation axis. Existing Opal known-compound interpolation has positive development evidence, but endpoint dose extrapolation failed. Public sci-Plex-GxE can support anchor-pair dose work after resolving physical semantics; unrelated time points cannot be pooled into nominal replicate cultures. Sequential and simultaneous interventions need separate contracts.

**Multimodal/pretraining** offers possible information, not guaranteed gains. Frozen representations from scVI/STATE or genetic foundation models require exposure checks and all-resource accounting. Baseline RNA/control counts may be enough; add chromatin/proteomics only when an ablation and crossed design can attribute their incremental value. No inspected public resource establishes broad matched pair × state × multimodal independent replication.

## Minimum public precursor and laboratory handoff

For the first public test, qualify an exact same-dose treatment/control panel from Tahoe using metadata only; select contexts/drugs by support, never response strength. Stream a bounded subset, preserve one plate-matched reference draw across its dependent contrasts, exclude the repeated plate from both evaluation leakage and exaggerated independent counts, and write condition manifests before aggregating expression. Hold complete lines in outer folds and complete lines inside selection. Interventions remain source-observed in this first experiment; unseen drug identity is a different subsequent test. Compare equal-information intervention means, low-rank context×drug matrix regression, nearest-control context transfer and control-conditioned ridge/kernel. Fix the total fit menu and output basis; compare frozen learned representations, trainable representation and shuffled context inputs without changing data. Report per-line/condition losses and a context-cluster interval as conditional retrospective uncertainty, not independent biological confirmation. A consistent material gain over the strongest simple reference plus loss of gain after context shuffling warrants pair-panel design, not a frontier claim. No gain stops this representation branch before pair or large-model scaling.

The laboratory contract should cross the same diverse pair panel through several **pretreatment-defined** contexts and independent culture initiations with randomized matched vehicle, both exact singles and pair in every unit. Start with a variance pilot that estimates shared-control/single covariance and pair-specific replicate reliability; choose numbers of cultures, contexts, pairs and cells from empirical power against the scientifically useful margin. Include shared-correction negative controls and blinded repeat pairs. A model cannot discover reproducible pair information if assay/reference reliability leaves that information unmeasured. This is a collaborator/quote dependency, not something a coding agent can create.

The strategically important capability is predicting an unseen pair in a held context using declared measured reference singles, with reproducible pair-specific effects beyond shared calibration. It is narrower than universal virtual-cell simulation but more consequential than another same-context MSE reduction. If the public single-transfer precursor fails, retain Opal as reference and pursue an independent pair-reliability pilot rather than treating all biological context as disproved. If the pilot finds no repeatable incremental pair signal at a useful scale, change assay/task before building another residual model.

## Actual cross-examination and revised recommendation

I read A's landscape report and F's systems report, including their recommendations and F's completed revision, and the root's `docs/frontier/experiment_contract.md`. The initial investigation above predates those reports; the following is the revised decision and preserves the remaining disagreement.

**A review.** A correctly identifies that generic context representation is now occupied by State/Stack/PIE/PRESAGE/TxPert, and that a repeated globally unseen pair/held-context capability would be more distinct. Its strongest recommendation initially conflates two unresolved requirements: whether measured pair departures repeat, and whether their structure transfers. Reproducible pair signals need independent cultures; the already exposed TNF banks cannot certify that. A source-average same-pair residual would also violate globally excluded pair identity if it uses the actual query pair anywhere in source outcomes. A's useful source correspondence control must therefore operate on source fitting pairs and source-learned modes, never on held query pair responses. The root bridge contract resolves this by excluding each query pair in all contexts and fitting two source modes before revealing target bridges. The remaining unsupported premise is that these modes retain transferable pair information after simple calibration; its null and nontransferable synthetic cases plus empirical alignment controls are essential, not optional figures.

A materially changes my priority assessment: broad public single-context transfer is technically more feasible, but has weaker strategic distinctness given contemporary native competitors. I retain Tahoe as a separate fallback task, not as an obligatory precursor or pair proof. A's landscape search cannot establish that no competitor already supports bridge adaptation; the day-7 native capability audit must check few-shot/context-conditioning protocols, not only zero-shot scores, and clearly label any pair adaptation.

**F review.** F correctly identifies the decisive information difference as acquired target pair measurements and audits the real 75.2 MB inherited artifact headroom. My original Tahoe proposal did not translate the dataset's streaming option into a byte-qualified executable storage plan; I revise it to require an explicit metadata manifest, size budget and separately scoped resource decision before any new download. The public 429-GB card is availability evidence, never a workstation acquisition recommendation.

F's original 0/8/16/32 prefixes, order seeds, six-value operator and nested target selection would combine label-budget and capacity unknowns while exceeding existing small common-pair support. Its revision shrinks the operator but uses three acquired scalar values, whereas the root contract uses two target coefficients and a G-dimensional shared offset. That is a genuine design difference: **the root's adapter has G+2 target fitted values, not two or three**, although all calibration controls receive the same G-dimensional intercept. I support the root formulation because identical offsets leave only the pair-dependent coefficients as the tested addition; resource and freedom counts must remain explicit. No claim that hundreds of genes supply hundreds of independent bridge observations is valid. Four bridge pairs can test this fixed regularized numerical adapter with reported effective rank; they cannot support target hyperparameter search or a general biological sample-size argument.

**Revised dominant first experiment.** Given the finite cached-data scope and stronger prior-art competition, I support the root's fixed four-bridge, two-mode transfer experiment as the immediate Opal composition test. Metadata qualification must establish at least 36 exact canonical common pairs and 256 reference-selected shared genes in the TNF banks before scoring. Those are feasibility thresholds, not empirical power or new cultures. Fail qualification once, preserve an infeasible result, and move to the independent crossed-panel contract; do not browse favorable outcomes until some public bank passes. The proposed 5% improvement in both directions is an administrative spending threshold. The directions are one exposed context contrast, not independent confirmations. A direction-specific failure must stop this finite operator branch, but it does not prove that a richer crossed context regime cannot work.

The highest-value **new information source** remains a collaborator-produced crossed pair/context panel with independent initiation. A public positive bridge result is sufficient to justify designing that panel, but cannot justify costly scaling alone. It must beat the same-budget offset, affine, incidence, target-only and pooled-kernel alternatives plus alignment controls, so new labels cannot be mistaken for a new biological capability. The independent measurement pilot must establish repeatable pair structure beyond those corrections before expensive architecture work. Exactly matched guides/reagents and pretreatment population definitions matter; unknown guide mixtures restrict the public result to its assignment-level contract.

**Preserved disagreement.** I still assign higher probability to a successful broad single-response transfer study than to a biologically confirmed pair/context operator, because Tahoe has much stronger crossed support and current Opal pair results are consistently unfavorable. I no longer choose it first for the stated frontier **combination** ambition: its success would change the task and face strong current native competitors. If the bridge experiment is precisely negative but an independently repeated pair pilot shows meaningful signal, invest in the crossed data rather than declaring all context information useless. If no useful repeatable pair signal survives qualified measurement, Tahoe becomes a distinct single-response pivot with native State/PIE and simple transfer baselines; its success cannot retrospectively rescue the failed combination hypothesis.
