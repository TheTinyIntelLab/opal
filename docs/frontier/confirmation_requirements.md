# Independent bridge confirmation requirements

This is a measurement qualification checklist for a future `frontier_bridge_confirmation_v1` contract. It is not a registered experiment or an executable confirmation protocol. The public prototype's failure to meet common-gene support does not authorize changing its endpoint or acquiring another public source.

Before creating that contract, retain reviewed evidence for independently initiated cultures nested within pretreatment-defined contexts, randomized plate/block assignment, literal matched intervention/reagent/dose/time assignments, simultaneous or sequential treatment, matched independently measured references, and release rights. SampleIndex is a technical stratum and cannot supply independent culture provenance.

Freeze globally excluded pair identities across every source context, culture and assignment-equivalent record, allowed query singles/controls, fixed bridge membership, and the full held query population before expression scoring. Charge distinct bridge identities across the campaign, each repeated stratum/culture outcome, and controls/singles. Four bridges per adaptation does not mean four acquired outcomes in the entire study.

A collaborator must set the useful response/program margin and review empirical covariance, power, sample size, assay cost and the functional endpoint. The illustrative workload in the active plan is not a powered sample size. Qualified culture data and reviewed empirical power are missing today, so context/culture counts, useful margin and final sampling design remain unset.

Validate interval coverage and null type-I error while retaining shared-reference covariance before confirmation scoring. Fixed-panel culture effects and inference over context/target populations require different support. Do not pass a mixed-context bank to the existing fixed-state replicated evaluator or claim conformal coverage under arbitrary shift.

Independent biological confirmation and frontier claims remain false until those measurements and claim-specific comparisons qualify. External registration, collaborator contact, paid compute, laboratory execution and redistribution require their actual owner arrangements.
