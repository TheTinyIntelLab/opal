## Reproduced scientific path

The crossed-single source SHA, corrected primary metadata SHA and registered preparation/model/tool identities match. Retained preparation yields 96 selected compounds and the original 512-gene panel. Each observed target has training identities and same-compound measurements from the other two lines. Reconstructed outer and inner allocations agree exactly with stored roles; each target query appears once. No evaluation response enters the fitting function. All ten families, 22 specifications, nine directions/folds and 684 invocations are accounted for. All 594 inner validation losses agree to numerical tolerance; every selected final penalty and locked family agrees; all 90 final predictions replay bitwise. Eleven prediction arms per direction include the selector. There are no unsupported/fallback rows in this actual run. All reported MSEs and seeded 2,000-draw conditional bounds reproduce exactly.

The candidate gains over the selector are 2.600%, 6.737% and 2.371% for A549, K562 and MCF7. Only K562 exceeds the 5% point-gain filter, but its conditional paired lower bound is negative. MCF7's difference ridge has lower MSE than the candidate. The finite spending filter fails in all directions. Compounds are paired computational comparison units; 96 compounds and three target directions are not 288 independent cultures. This tests observed-context missing-response prediction conditional on measured source singles and target training labels, not unseen identities, new contexts, interaction mechanism or biological confirmation. The only registered sensitivity is the coupled whole-compound resampling; no new rank, seed or dose was searched here.

GxE custody checks confirm five acquired files totaling 611,638,699 bytes. Headerless count axes and physical RT allocation agree with the inspected author scripts. Complete hash-table semantics, unique well lineage, source biological independence and redistribution permission remain unresolved. The panel uses controls/singles, with pair outcomes used for full-library count totals but not panel variance. That all-cell total pass is declared preprocessing; it prevents a stronger claim that no pair expression was accessed before the panel. Row parity provides disjoint technical cells. Shared reference means and one jointly selected reference panel couple the comparisons, so technical-half agreement is not an independent estimator of culture variability.

All 66 pair/origin rows remain in the population ledger, including 19 unsupported rows. Supported means reproduce from `metrics.json`, with 18/19/10 supported rows by origin. Residual-half error exceeds zero residual in every origin. This is descriptive agreement under one supported-panel/filter, without a significance test, biological interval or general biological negative. Source-side exclusions and changed RT/schema/reference-weight preparation attempts remain preserved. Counts were not reprocessed to fill the missing aggregate custody or produce a favorable result.
