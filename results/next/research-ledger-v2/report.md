The two native adaptations use the original model and loss implementations with aggregate observations. GEARS uses graphs derived only from permitted single profiles rather than external GO. These results do not support a canonical state-of-the-art comparison. CPA has a reproducible weight/reference export with an exact no-label restore.

D-SPIN supplies a second study with matched doses and within-batch vehicle references. Whole Myeloid and NK lineages are held out across both batches. Published lineage labels were assigned after treatment, dosage pairs share four compounds, and batches are not biological replicates. The frozen procedure fails the 5% skill gate.

The estimated-single route uses a separate schema, never marks estimates as measured, reports the source of each single, and abstains when an exact intervention/dose has no source reference. Observed-single intervals never apply to it.

The next scientific step is a replicated crossed-context screen or an independently qualified corpus, followed by a fresh sealed confirmation. Conditional learned encoders, higher-order interventions, sequential treatment and mechanistic claims remain outside the demonstrated release.
